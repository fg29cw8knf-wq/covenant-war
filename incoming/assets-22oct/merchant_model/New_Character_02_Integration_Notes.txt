  wave) derived from a 34-joint animation model with EXACT matching rig hierarchy.
- 10 extra clips (sneak, strafe_left, fight_stance, punch_combo, roundhouse,
  flying_kick, sword_swing, kata, hit_react, roll) transferred from 52-joint
  Player B by anatomical mapping of its torso, limbs, and the two equivalent

- Rest-pose-relative quaternion motion preserved for each mapped joint;
  rest-pose-adjusted translations and scales account for character proportions.
- Original 3D mesh, embedded textures, rig hierarchy, skin weights, inverse
  bind matrices, static rest transforms and original GLB data were not replaced.
