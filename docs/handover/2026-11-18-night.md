## What's honest to say about 4 and 5

- **4.** The models have no mouth inside and no ring of points round the
  lips (a split along the lips came out as a jagged black notch). What's
  there now is drawn by the shader a pixel at a time: the skin the jaw
  draws out between the lips goes dark, with the upper teeth's edge. It
  reads at the game's distances and in a face's close-up; it isn't teeth
  and a tongue. The way to those is the models remade with a jaw (Tripo,
  Mixamo or AccuRig face rigs), which is his to do.
- **5.** The hero's finger bones (index, middle, ring, little) all sit at
  his knuckles, the index's tip bone 5 cm off his hand, and his skin is
  weighted to them every which way (one index bone moves 557 points of the
  hand). That's why his fingers were frozen. Re-rigging them here, blind,
  would risk his hands everywhere in the game. So the hand is closed by the
  shader: each hand's knuckle line and the fingers' way were measured off
  his skin (`Figure3D.HANDS`), and everything beyond it bends round towards
  the palm. The four fingers bend together, without joints; past 0.8 the
  tips fold into the palm (`HAND_MOST`). Holding cards and the crate wasn't
  done: curled, the fingers go into what they hold (the crate's sides are
  where the palms face).
