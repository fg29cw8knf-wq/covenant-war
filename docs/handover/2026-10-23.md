## Later the same night: the cutscenes' look, and Gary's address

Gary's reply to the films plan: he'd rather keep the two cutscenes in the
game and make them look better ("they don't look very good yet"). The story
films stay built for whenever he wants them; what changed in the engine:

- **A film's own light**: every shot (`Cine.shot`, `move`, `drift`) now sets a
  soft warm key light beside the camera on the subject (`Cine.light_shot`;
  0.55 in Ashford's nights, 0.25 by day and elsewhere), a close shot (fov
  40 or tighter) gets depth of field on its subject, on a phone too, and the
  movie bars bring a vignette (`CineOverlay.VIGNETTE`). All of it goes with
  `end_cine` / `cine_home` (`Cine.end_light`).
- **The Sigilfall's rift**: the sky's tear read as a lightning bolt; it now
  opens into a wide crack with its colours spilling below it as `tear_open`
  rises (`sky.gdshader`), and `tear_light` lights the village from above as
  it opens. Seen in Forward+ here: a broad teal-violet rift over the hill.
- **The stand-in cut-outs** (children, Ned, the elder, the refugee) are lit by
  the scene (`PaperDoll` `shaded`) and lost their white sticker border
  (`DollArt._svg`), so they sit in the dark instead of glowing. They are still
  cartoons among painted figures: Gary's models for them are the biggest
  single lift left, and on his list.
- Forward+ can be rendered here after all: `apt-get install
  mesa-vulkan-drivers` gives lavapipe, then
  `VK_ICD_FILENAMES=/usr/share/vulkan/icd.d/lvp_icd.json xvfb-run ... godot
  --rendering-driver vulkan --rendering-method forward_plus --resolution
  960x540 ... --phone` renders the iPhone's look (level 1). It is slow (a
  still every minute or two) and takes 3.5 GB: run nothing else beside it,
  or the container's memory limit kills it. Before/after stills of the
  midnight talk and the rift were compared this way.
- **Gary's address** for reports, `garymannion@123mail.org`, is on the
  notice's second page (`NoticeScreen.CONTACT`) and the Options page.
