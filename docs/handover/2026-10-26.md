## Tools

- `tools/reskin.py NEW.glb REF.glb OUT.glb --height H`: a new mesh onto the
  game's skeleton (REF's joints moved by the offset between REF's flesh and the
  nearest points of the new mesh; weights by distance to the bones, the nearest
  strongly, smoothed over the mesh, seam copies alike; the root bone moves
  nothing). Then `rig_character.py OUT.glb ... --borrow REF.glb:<its clips>`.
  Use it for any Tripo3D person whose rig is no good; the apron's bib lifts a
  little with Bram's arm when he waves, the only artefact seen.
- `tools/split_creature.py in.glb assets/models/creatures/<id>.glb [--glow] [--tone 0.85]`.
