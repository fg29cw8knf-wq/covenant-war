# Handover, 18 November 2026: the arena's look, and all seven every update

Read `README.md` first: the standing rule's new part, "And all seven, every
update (18 November)", and "The arena's look (18 November)" in the Duel Lab.
Gary is non-technical: plain, short English. Mac and iPhone builds only.
After every build he gets numbered suggestions he can answer "N yes".

Gary (18 November) asked how to get the game looking like "other games you
see on Steam, like the most recent Final Fantasys". He was told the seven
things that make the difference (lighting, materials, animation, camera,
effects, colour grade, consistency), said yes to a look pass on the duel's
arena first, and: "I want you to add a rule to every game update to make
all [of those] are done so that everything runs at the best possible look".
That rule is in the README now (top, under the standing rule) and in
Claude's memory for the project.

One session, one worker; no graphics card (Forward+ through lavapipe,
`VK_ICD_FILENAMES=/usr/share/vulkan/icd.d/lvp_icd.json` after `apt-get
install mesa-vulkan-drivers`). Godot 4.7.2 downloaded and the project
imported from cold (about 40 minutes on two cores).

## Things that are mine, not his (he can change any)

- **How strong each part is**: gentle on purpose (the README's "a touch,
  never plastic"): AO 2.4, haze 0.012, floor normal 0.7, skin 0.55, the coats'
  leftover warmth 0.22.
- **The grades**: Emberforge ember gold, Tidegrove teal shadows, Veilwild
  violet, Ironstone warm stone and cool shade, Solhaven as the town's,
  Ashford lantern-lit green.
- **The coats**: fourteen Totems' bodies glowed with their own paint at 0.8
  (`coat_emission`, `cream_emission`, `gold_emission`). Now they keep 0.22
  and the stage lights them: they have shape, but they're darker in the dark.
  If Gary finds one hard to make out, raise `LIT_GLOW` (or turn `lit` off).
- **On the iPhone (Smooth)**: the small shadows, the floor and skin maps,
  the coats, the reflection and the grade; no bounced light, no haze (frame
  rate). "Full" on the iPhone gets everything.

## Things worth knowing

- **The Totems' atlases are hundreds of islands**: a normal map filtered as
  one picture ridges every island's edge. `derive_creature_maps.py` finds the
  islands from the model's UVs and works inside each.
- **Gary's own Tripo3D models came with their maker's normal and
  roughness maps**; `tools/split_creature.py` drops them. His originals would
  give the Tripo Totems (Cinderpup, Brookfin, River Hound, Kaethis's four,
  Shellguard, Orlanthe) truer maps than the derived ones.
- **Volumetric fog lit by a big flash floods the picture**: the flash lights
  are 5 to 6 over 7 to 14 m. Any new light in the duel should set
  `light_volumetric_fog_energy` (`HAZE_FLASH` for anything brief and bright).
- **The haze needs `sky_affect` 1** in the arena: with 0 the black beyond the
  scenery painting had none and the painting's top edge was a hard line.
- **The Ironhide's and the Brassback's brass** was mostly glow, not metal:
  the reflection only shows on them now that `lit` is on.
