## The frame rate: what was done, and what's a guess

- Nothing was measured on a Mac. `frame_cost_probe` says the game's own
  scripts cost a millisecond a frame; the rest is the picture.
- The guess: under "Best picture" the frame-rate guard stood down by
  design, so an M1 drew every pixel of a Retina screen with the bounce
  light, SSIL, SSAO, the haze and 2x MSAA on: 15 to 19 is about what that
  costs.
- What changed: the guard works under "Best picture" too. It draws fewer
  of the 3D's pixels and rebuilds the rest with MetalFX Temporal (what the
  iPhone build has always done at two-thirds size), and under "Best
  picture" it never takes any of the light away. Stills of the rebuilt
  picture at two-thirds (FSR2 standing in for MetalFX, which only a Mac
  has) showed no ghosting on the walk in the snow.
- From the engine's source: MetalFX Temporal only exists on Metal; it
  falls back to MetalFX Spatial, then FSR2, where it isn't supported or the
  size is outside what it allows (`rebuild_least` asks; on Vulkan that
  number is rubbish, so it's only asked where `SUPPORTS_METALFX_TEMPORAL`);
  the engine turns MSAA off under it (it does its own).
- If the speed test says something else is the cost (the lamps' shadows,
  the grass), that's the next thing; "GI at half size" is there in `Gfx`
  (`gi_half`) and off until it's measured.

## Things worth knowing (traps met today)

- **One copy of the game at a time on this machine.** Two drawn runs at
  once ran it out of memory (8 GB) and the first chapter's sweep was killed
  23 minutes in. `flock /tmp/godot.lock` is there for that.
- **A drawn pose is only in the bones inside `skeleton_updated`** (or a
  SkeletonModifier3D). Read anywhere else, `palm_frame()` and the bones are
  the clip's, before any reach: the button clasp "measured" 42 cm apart
  that way, with the hands drawn 1 cm apart.
- **A hand's mark isn't its palm.** The reach's target is on the line of
  the forearm; a palm turned down at the wrist lies about 4 cm off it. The
  clasp brings the palm onto the other palm by reading both as drawn.
- **The body check read carried things a frame late** (fixed: see the
  README). Its earlier "hand in the bundle" and "fingers in the crate" at a
  walk were that. `held_child_probe ... arms` and `crate_take_probe` read
  both in one pass.
- **`ShardSet.weather()` needs to know what a model is painted with.** The
  afternoon's normal maps put Bram's coat in the people's shader, and
  `weather()` only knew StandardMaterial3D: his coat lost its wind and its
  snow, silently (it returned null). It reads either now. Anything else
  that swaps a model's material for its own needs the same.
- **`Figure3D.FROZEN`**: Player A's finger bones are frozen (his skin
  doesn't follow them). His hand can't close on anything.
- **The models have no mouth.** No jaw bone, no face shapes, no inside. A
  split along the lips with a dark inside was tried: these meshes have no
  ring of edges round the mouth and it came out as a jagged black notch.
  What's there is the lower lip and chin moving on the painted face.
- **A blanket laid over him has his shape in it**: thrown to the wall like
  that, the heap had legs. The heap is the blanket as it was laid by rule.
- **A cloth that lies into every hollow catches the window's light in
  patches** (a mound over each hand). It's drawn tauter down the bed than
  across him (`TIGHT_ALONG`).
