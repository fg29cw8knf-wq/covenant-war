## The phone link: no longer kept up (Gary, 2 November, 01:43)

"Don't worry about doing the phone link, just the Mac version and the one
for the phone through Xcode." So no more web builds or publishes: the Mac
app (`mac` release, CovenantWar-Mac.zip) and the iPhone app's Xcode project
(`iphone` release, CovenantWar-iOS.zip) rebuild themselves on every push to
`main`; both were rebuilt at 01:30 GMT from `da360cb`, with everything below.
The size question under Before launch is gone with it. Gary then asked
whether the shrunk textures would be better bigger: yes, so the Golden
Gate's atlas is back at 4096 (VRAM-compressed: 10.7 MB in memory) and the
hay cart's, the first merchant cart's, the stall's and the well's at 2048.
People and creatures stay at 2048: their textures are lossy (decoded to
full RGBA in memory, about 21 MB each at 2048, 85 MB at 4096) and twenty-odd
can be on screen in Ashford at once, too much for a phone at 4096. A 4096
that fits would be ASTC (VRAM-compressed) on the iPhone, about the memory of
today's 2048 but a much bigger app: part of the look pass, to ask Gary
before starting.
