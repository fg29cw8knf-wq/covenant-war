## What Gary asked for tonight, and what was done

- **The opening notice cut off by itself.** `NoticeScreen` moved on after seven
  seconds on every launch but the first, and any tap on the page dismissed it.
  Now it waits for Continue (or the interact key / pad button); no timer, no
  tap-anywhere (`scripts/ui/notice_screen.gd`).
- **Asking more than one thing in a conversation.** A new dialogue step,
  `{"ask": [questions], "then": [[...]...], "leave": "..."}` (`World._ask`):
  after each answer the questions not yet asked come back, with the leave
  line last when there is one, until the leave line is picked or nothing's
  left; a single question left is offered on its own. A plain answer to what
  someone's just said or asked stays `"choice"` (one pick, two ways). Converted:
  Bram in the taproom ("What happens when it ends?" / "I dreamt about the sky
  again."), Bram handing over the first deck ("Why now?" / "Mine? To keep?"),
  Corin before the Lantern Duel ("What's that letter?", leave "Let's duel."),
  Bram's wagon menu in Solhaven (five things, leave "That's all.") and Old
  Maren ("Wrong how?" / "Why tell me?"). The film forks (`Cine.choose`) and the
  side stories' decisions are story forks and were left as they are.
  `tests/ashford_test.gd` and `tests/solhaven_test.gd` walk the lists.
- **The breather line names the menu.** Bram's line at the door now ends
  "(Tap the three lines at the top right of the screen to open the menu.)" on a
  phone or tablet, "(Press Esc to open the menu.)" on a computer, "(Press Start
  to open the menu.)" on a pad: `{menu}` in a line is filled by `World._fmt`
  from `WorldCoach.MENU_HOW` for the device in hand, as the Objective line's
  brackets are. Noted in `docs/script/v3_prologue_p1.md`; not in Gary's master
  doc.
- **Three more Champion faces**: Magister Tobias Crane (the scholar with the
  floating books), Sister Maelis (the blindfold) and Silk (the mask) ->
  `assets/art/champions/crane.jpg`, `maelis.jpg`, `silk.jpg` (768 px, same
  import settings as the others). Only Aldric's is still the Seat's sign.
- **All four looks in the mirror.** Gary's three new models are looks B, C
  and D (`tools/rig_player.sh b|c|d <master>` -> `player_b.glb`, `player_c.glb`,
  `player_d.glb`; B 1.76 m, C 1.68 m, D 1.82 m; paces in `Figure3D.OWN_GAIT`;
