   and throws it back across him to the wall, the cloth following the hand
   (`scripts/world/blanket_throw.gd`: the blanket keeps its grid of points
   from `InnRooms._blanket_points`, the corner on the hand, the near edge
   moving first and the far edge last, a mesh rebuilt each frame); then the
   hand goes down on the mattress and he pushes up (`sit_long`). He slides
   to the bed's edge with his legs still out along the mattress
   (`Figure3D.shift_seat`, now with a `lift`) and only then swings them
   down over the edge (`sit`), so nothing goes through the bed.
   `tests/bed_check.tscn` (it taps lines now) takes stills from the side
   and the foot: looked at, the throw reads and the legs stay above the
   mattress. The real fix is a clip from Gary: asked for on the Still to

   sits, stands", the shared skeleton, as Corin's walk).

   people standing about as blockers too (`Cine.people`: the player, the

   when the player comes within 0.6 m (`AshfordP1.FOLLOW_SPACE`).
   `tests/route_test.tscn` has a standing-figure case: ok.

   voiced line's timing (`films.gd _voice`, `Ashford._herald_say`, both pass

   line left waiting swallowed every key after it (32 of its checks failed
