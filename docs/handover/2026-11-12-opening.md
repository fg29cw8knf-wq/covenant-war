## The opening, as it runs now

First launch: notice, the opening movie (skippable), title. Every launch
after: notice, title. New Game: the snow at once (Ashford is still built
under the title). The snow: caption, walk, crater, the child, the hunter,
then the duel: the Warg breaks the Scrivener; (tap) the Unspent Hour is
called; (tap) it breaks the Blazehound; the pack breaks the Loomwarden;
(tap) the Last Page; white; "Twenty years later, the debt came due."; the
attic. `white_sky_test` headless: duel begins 53 s in, first card 60 s, the
end 84 s, with the test's quick hands.

The duel is `WhiteSkyDuel.PLAY_TURNS` on `play_board()`. To change what
happens, change those and run `tests/white_sky_play_probe.gd` (it checks
each Totem breaks on the turn written and no step is skipped). The numbers
that make it work: Scrivener hurt 110 of 180, Loomwarden 100 of 220,
Blazehound 40 of 120; Hellfire Charge does 100, Turn the Glass 90, Mirage
50, Final Entry 140.
