## Things worth knowing

- **Nobody has heard any of it.** The sounds were checked as numbers and
  pictures (levels, lengths, spectrograms), the lines by a speech-to-text
  pass (each says its words; the two cuts fall in the quiet between takes).
  How it all sits together in the mix is for Gary's ears.
- **`light_wave` is almost all deep bass** (most of it under 80 Hz). On a
  phone's speaker it will hardly be heard; on headphones or a Mac it's a
  deep boom. The game's rush (`cast_wind`) plays over it for that reason.
- **`ice_ring_3`** came 20 dB quieter than the other two, with its left and
  right sides against each other; it's been brought up and its sides
  brought together. It may sound thinner than the other two.
- **The stand-ins were quiet.** His tune, mark and Circle sounds come out 7
  to 9 dB louder than the made-up ones did, and the ice ring 8 dB louder,
  at the same settings in the code: they're now at the level of every other
  effect. The made-up crack and shatter were hotter than the game's other
  effects; his are 7 and 11 dB under them, in line with the rest.
- **The morning's people's-normal-maps build broke Bram's weather**
  (`ShardSet.weather` returned null for a body wearing
  `character_nm.gdshader`), and nothing that morning ran the whole
  `white_sky_test` after it. Anything else that reads a person's
  `StandardMaterial3D` could have the same blind spot: `grep` for
  `as StandardMaterial3D` near `get_active_material` before giving another
  body a normal map.
- **A Tripo3D "metal" isn't always metal.** Its maps are made for a bright
  studio light. `add_maker_maps.py --metal` is there for the next model
  that goes black under a night sky; look at every restored model in an
  arena (`duel_look_shot ... mine=`), not only at its numbers.
- **The look back's hill is steep** (a metre in three, and askew to the
  village). Anything else set on it wants seating as `_seat_bank` does, or
  it floats at one corner.
- **Importing here is slow** (two cores, no graphics card): a first import
  of the whole project is about an hour, a 4K picture about a minute.
  Swapping model files in and out for a before and after costs minutes
  each way.
- Gary's Mac's folders were reachable for part of the session only; nothing
  was written back to them.
