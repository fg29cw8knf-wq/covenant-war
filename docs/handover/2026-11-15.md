## Things worth knowing

- **A moon "straight down" turns the sky black.** `sky.gdshader` takes
  `cross(moon_dir, up)`; with the moon at (0, -1, 0) that's a zero vector,
  its `normalize` is not-a-number, and so is every pixel of the sky: black,
  with bloom's white round everything that stands against it. The moon is
  put down behind him at an angle (`_first_light`). The same family as the
  14th's `SHAFT` bug: a NaN pixel is black and bloom makes it a white glow.
- **A lambda keeps a copy of a local number.** In the look test's "out"
  view a clock `t` read inside a lambda stayed 0, so every still "after N
  seconds" was never taken. The clock is a member (`_tt`).
- **The walk's camera is a spring behind him**, so on the bowl's steep side
  it hangs low: a close shot aimed at something by his shoulder there has
  it in the top of the picture. The small hand waits until he's over the
  lip (`HAND_AT`).
- **The held mark belongs to the world**, not to the memory (which is freed
  before the attic's caption). It has a `life` so that a memory played by
  something that never lets it go doesn't leave it on the screen: `_close`
  gives it 20 seconds; the attic lets it go long before.
- **The hero's mark is under his shirt.** `SealMark.show_on` can't show it
  where he lies asleep. `through` draws it without a depth test for the
  attic's first shot, and `go_out` fades its alpha with its light and then
  puts both back; `blaze(w, 0)` there left an ash-grey stamp drawn through
  the blanket as the camera drew back.
- **The camera's way back from the mark** is a little way straight back,
  then through the dark to the bed's own shot. One move round to that shot
  was tried twice: as two fixed views blended (`interpolate_with`) it
  looked at the rug half way; with its eyes kept on him it passed close
  over where his hands and waistcoat come through the blanket, a thing too
  small to see from the bed's shot. (That poke-through is there in the
  bed's shot too, small: not from this, not fixed.) The mark's shot has a
  film's bars on it (`CineOverlay.bars`), off again in the dark before the
  bed's shot: the lower one covers a sliver of his cuff that comes through
  the blanket at the foot of so close a picture.
- **`_stage_duellists` is called twice** when the flashes are shown (by
  `_flashes`, then `_duel`): it only moves what isn't already there.
- **The Last Page is no longer the last buzz of the phone**: the small
  hand's tap is. The test watches for the Last Page's long one as it goes by.
- **`white_sky_test ... duel`** now sets `_ad` and `_bottom` itself (nobody
  knelt to the child) and plays the walk out and the end; the quick way to
  see the ending. `skip_out=<s>` presses Skip that long into the walk out:
  the Summon's video before it runs by the wall's clock headless, so the
  walk out's place in the test's own time isn't known beforehand.

## The review Gary asked for, and what's left before he marks the prologue done

Gary (15 November, while this was building): review the prologue from a few
different players' points of view (will they love it, think it pointless,
not understand it, want more), compare it with games and stories of its
kind, and say what's left to polish so he can mark it done and move on.
It was given to him in the conversation, from the script, the stills and
the timings, not from anyone playing it. In short:

- **What works:** the spine is clear without a word of explanation (a man
  finds a marked child, something faceless comes for it, he fights with
  cards and gets away, twenty years pass, you are the child); the player
  does the walking and the three moves himself; it now ends quietly and
  hands the mark on to the hero.
- **The biggest gap:** the debt. Morrowen opens and closes on it and
  nothing shows what it is or whose. Idea 1 (the price in Morrowen's book)
  or a line would close it.
- **The risks:** a first-time player sits through Gary's opening film
  (3 min 15 s, skippable) and then this (about 2 min 45 s, not skippable
  the first time) before the attic, and plays his first real duel later
  still; the played duel has no choice in it; quitting half way starts it
  again from the top with no Skip.
