## Shards (a fork's work, checked and merged)

See the README line. Numbers: a won story duel spills 10% of its Resonance (at
least 1, doubled by the win's advert); six Sigilfall crystals round Solhaven's
square, 2 or 3 each, back every calendar day, 12 a day at most; side stories
pay once (Mercy 20, Fen 15, Pip 15, the Sundial 20, Hale 25); a spare copy of a
Totem with nothing left to manifest and fully Tempered turns into a tenth of its
cost. Forged from the Codex, any time; no Rank experience from forging. Pacing
(`shards_test`): Shards add about 12% to a chapter's cards for a player who only
follows the story, 27% for one who does the side stories and comes back on 3
days, 33% for one who comes back every day for a week. Not done: Sigilfall spots
outside Solhaven (later chapters add theirs to `SigilShards.SPOTS`), back-pay
for side stories finished in old saves, and one look at the spots in Gary's real
square.
