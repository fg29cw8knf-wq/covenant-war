## The creature voices

`Sfx.cry_of(id, when)` returns `[sound, pitch, dB]`. It looks for, in turn,
`cry_<id>_<when>`, `cry_<id>`, `cry_<kind>_<when>`, `cry_<kind>` in
`assets/sfx/` (`.ogg`, `.wav`, `.mp3`), `when` being `call`, `attack`, `hurt`
or `ko`. The kind is `Sfx.VOICE_OF[id][0]`, else the family's
(`VOICE_OF_FAMILY`).

| Kind | Stand-in | Creatures |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hound` | a growl into a bark (hurt and fall: Gary's `hound_whine`) | Cinderpup, Blazehound, Infernal Warg, Shieldhound, River Hound, Hallowed Hound |
| `fox` | two yips | Sparkkit, Dreamfox, Veilfox, Ninefold Fox, Starling |
| `cat` | a hiss into a snarl | Voltlynx, Tempest Lynx, Sunmane Lion, Duskstalker |
| `sprite` | a run of small bells, wings | Lantern Moth, Dawnling, Bloomsprite, Emberwisp, Mossling |
| `owl` | two hoots | Somnowl, Runeowl |
| `bird` | a squawk | Stormcaller |
| `serpent` | a hiss with a rattle | Brookfin, Riptide Serpent, Plague Asp, Blight Wyrm, Hearth Salamander, Weft Serpent |
| `drake` | a roar | Ashen Drake, Deepmaw |
| `shell` | clicks and a hollow knock | Shellguard, Brassback, Ironhide, Ironclad Behemoth, Mirewidow |
| `stone` | a grind and a thud | Pebblit, Boulderox, Cragmaul, Quarry Golem, Gilded Sentinel, Glyph Warden |
| `wood` | a creak, leaves | Briar Colossus, Elderbark, Thornwarden |
| `rodent` | three squeaks | Blightrat, Burrowmole |
| `beast` | a snort and a grunt | Rimecub, Thunder Ram, Thought Eater |
| `astral` | glass singing, pages | Scrivener, Loomwarden, Unspent Hour, Sunlance Seraph |

The six Summons each have a cry of their own since later the same day
(below).

The stand-ins are made by `python3 tools/make_cries.py [kind ...]` (numpy and
scipy; `--out <dir>` to try one somewhere else). Each is levelled so its
loudest fifth of a second matches the others, under a peak of 0.8. **Nobody
has heard them**: they were checked as pictures (spectrograms: the bark's
falling pitch, the two yips, the hoots, the bells and so on are there) and by
their levels. Expect some to need changing once Gary has heard them: change
the recipe and rerun, or drop his own file in under the same name.

How a cry is pitched and how loud: `Sfx.SIZE_PITCH` (by Health: 1.24 at 50
down to 0.72 at 200 and over), the nudge in `VOICE_OF`, `CRY_WHEN` (attack
x1.1 and 4 dB down, hurt x1.18 and 5 dB down, fall x0.78 and 1 dB down),
`CRY_DB` (3 dB under the effects), kept within `CRY_PITCH`. A recording made
for the moment (`..._ko`) plays as recorded.

**Summons.** `cry_dawnmane`, `cry_grondmaw`, `cry_pyraxis`, `cry_somnara`,
`cry_thalassa`, `cry_orlanthe` (`tools/make_cries.py`, the second half of

`DuelScreen.fx_summon`'s landing plays `_cry(card.id, "call",

as made. Heard in Bram's filmed duel too (Orlanthe), over the stinger.

**Footsteps.** `Figure3D._footfalls` (called from `_process` while
`walking`) reads the LeftFoot and RightFoot bones' heights above the
figure's ground, keeps each foot's lowest (`_step_rest`, eased up at
`STEP_EASE` so a new clip or a slope finds it again), and calls `_step`
when a foot that was `STEP_LIFT` up comes back within `STEP_DOWN`; never
two within `STEP_GAP`. `_step` takes the ground from `step_on`, else asks
the nearest ancestor with a `surface_at()` (the `World`; asked each time
because two worlds can exist at once, the title's and the one got ready
under it), emits `footfall(side, surface)` and calls `Sfx.step`. Only
`walk`, `run` and `sneak` clips count, and never during an action (the
kneel at the crater). `Sfx.step` plays `step_<ground>` on `Sfx.steps()`.

| Ground | Where | Stand-in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `snow` | Bram's memory (`WhiteSky`: `step_on`) | a creaking crunch |
| `grass` | Ashford and the Thornwood off the paths | a soft swish |
| `dirt` | their paths, the smugglers' tunnel, Bram's shelter | a dull thud, grit |
| `wood` | the taproom, the attic, the evening taproom, the mill, any other room | a hollow knock, sometimes a creak |
| `stone` | the cellar, Solhaven | a sharp tap, a scuff |

A new place with its own ground: give its area script a static
`surface_at(w, at) -> String` (return "" to leave it to the world's rule).
A new kind of ground: add `step_<name>.wav` (and `_2` ...); an unknown
name falls to `step_dirt`. Not done: footsteps for the 2D paper dolls of
the phone link; a splash for the river's edge; a different sound for the
inn's stairs.

Measured rates (`steps_test -- list`): the player's run 3.8 a second, walk
1.8; Bram in his coat at 1.1 m/s 1.0; Corin's run 2.8. The walk to the
crater in `white_sky_test`: 29 steps.

**Ambience** (`scripts/ui/ambience.gd`, one node under the root, three
players to ease between). It polls: `wanted()` asks each node in the group
`worlds` for `ambience()` and takes the last that answers. `World.ambience`
is "" when the world is `attract`, `held`, hidden, stopped
(`PROCESS_MODE_DISABLED`: how Main puts it behind a duel) or under a wholly
black screen; else the area's `ambience_at(w, w.cam_ground())`.
`Ambience.hush` above 0 silences everything (StoryFilm, WhiteSky). A bed
come back to starts from a random second of its loop.

| Bed | Where | Made of |
| --- | --- | --- |

Silent on purpose: the attic, Bram's shelter, Ashford on the dawn after the
fires. `bed_tavern` (a hearth, a room talking, mugs) is written in the tool
but not made: no room has a crowd in it yet (the evening taproom is Bram
and the player).

How loud: the loops are made quiet (`LOUD` 0.07 over the whole loop) and
played at `Ambience.DB` (-6) under the sound-effects volume. If Gary finds
them too quiet or too loud, `DB` is the one number.

**Stairs and water.** `World.surface_at` gives "stairs" for anywhere
indoors more than `STAIR_RISE` above the rooms' floor (`INDOOR_FLOOR`, -40:
every room is built there), before the area is asked;
`Thornwood.surface_at` gives "water" where the ground is under
`ThornwoodSet.WATER_Y`. Four takes of each from `tools/make_steps.py`.
Stairs are only climbed in staged walks (the player's body has no gravity),
so that's where they'll be heard.

Things found on the way that the next person should know:

- **The walking camera never sees the sky** (it looks down at 32 degrees
  whatever the zoom: the top of its picture is ground about 18 m past the

  end is brought down onto the green close by. From a low camera it is a
  whole arc.
- **The dawn is a staged scene, and the Herald's mist is a story beat**
  ("a mist comes down the road against the wind with a Pale Herald in

## The button click

`Sfx.watch_buttons(tree)` (called by `Game._ready`) connects every
`BaseButton` that enters the tree to `Sfx._click`, deferred, which plays
`ui_tap` on `Sfx.shared()` unless any sound was played in the last 90 ms
(the press's own handler got there first). `set_meta("quiet", true)` before
the button enters the tree keeps one silent. The shared player and every
`Sfx` now run while the tree is paused.

## The M1

Facts measured here (software rendering, so counts, not frame rates):

- Ashford as a Mac builds it: 858 MB of textures, 55 MB of meshes; with a
  duel over it 1,075 MB. A duel draws 0.2 million triangles in 580 calls.
- The village stood 4.3 million triangles, 2.0 million of them grass in one
  42,000-instance MultiMesh (drawn whole wherever the camera looked, 144
  unindexed points a clump, a sine and a noise per point in its vertex
  shader, in the depth pass and the colour pass).
- The Thornwood: every tree in eight MultiMeshes with shadows on, so every
  tree in every shadow cascade every frame.

Changed: `AshfordVillage.in_cells` sorts placings into squares;
`grass_field` (12 m) and `multimesh_cells` (28 m for the wood's trees) make
one MultiMesh a square. `blade_clump` is indexed. Before and after stills of
the same frame (`tests/tri_probe.tscn ... shot=`): the grass identical (the
only differing pixels are the people, who breathe), the wood identical to
the pixel. A frame from the green: 4.9 to 3.1 million triangles; from over
the camp: 9.0 to 2.7.

Not changed: `Gfx.Guard`, the detail levels, any setting. Gary said not to
lighten.
