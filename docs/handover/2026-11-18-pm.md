## Things worth knowing

- **The 2K AssetFurnace people can't take a derived map.** Their atlases are
  many small pieces with patchy paint; the map tilted each piece as a whole
  (faceted faces, bare arms, bodices). A finer filter made Bram's apron
  lumpy instead. Their patchy faces are the models' own (the same with no
  map). They wait for their 4K Tripo remakes (`PEOPLE_MIN_ATLAS` 4096).
- **Baked light (LightmapGI)**: Godot bakes only in its editor at a press of
  Bake (`bake()` isn't open to scripts) and only with a graphics card, and
  Ashford is built by code as it loads (no saved scene to bake). The Mac
  has SDFGI there; the iPhone on Smooth has no bounced light. Offered:
  VoxelGI for the taproom and the attic (worked out as the place loads,
  Forward+ on both).
- Kaethis's close-up in `cast_skin_shot` misses the face (2.3 m, hooded):
  its robes were judged instead.
