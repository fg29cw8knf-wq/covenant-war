## The memory's music

`WhiteSky.play` starts `white_sky` (the walk) from its beginning every time
(`Music.FROM_START`), the duel's spec names `white_sky_duel` first (so
`DuelScreen` crossfades to it as the duel opens: 2.8 s of hush, then the hit),
and `Music.stop(0.12)` runs as the white lands, so the climax stops dead with
it; `_close`'s `Music.stop(2.0)` then has nothing to fade. The walk loop's
join is the weakest of the five (rhythm 0.47: a slow build with little beat),
but the walk takes 60 to 90 s and the loop is at 122 s, so it's seldom heard.
