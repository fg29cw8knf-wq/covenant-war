## Notes for whoever is next

- A hand on a thing: `palm(side, dir, secs, flat, own_frame, fingers)`. Use
  `flat` 1.0 whenever a thin or flat thing is held (a box on a palm, a
  letter), or the clip's curled fingers go through it; give `fingers` when
  the forearm points at the thing (holding a box by its sides), or the hand
  folds at the wrist.
- `Figure3D.bone_pos` reads the clip's pose, before the hand IK: good for
  shoulders, heads and feet, not for a reaching hand.
- `_hand_can(f, side, to)` is the lying-down-safe `_in_reach`.
- `walk_room` zones: `stairs_zone` (a Rect2) and `stray_zones`
  (`[{"name", "rect", "back"}]`); `stray` is called with the name when it
  takes an argument.
