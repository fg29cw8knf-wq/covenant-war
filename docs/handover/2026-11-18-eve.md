## Findings worth keeping

- The editor ignores `override.cfg`; the script switches the plugin on in
  `project.godot` for the run and puts it back. An editor killed by hand
  rewrites `project.godot` without the iOS and renderer settings:
  `git checkout project.godot`.
- Import everything before the editor opens (the script does), or the bake
  can't import its own light picture ("reimport_files() recursively").
- Baked light adds to the sky's and the room's ambient light in Godot 4.7
  (it doesn't replace it): the attic with its old lights drew within 1% of
  the unbaked attic.
- The attic's dark wood gives back little light: the bake shapes it, the
  two stand-in lights stay.
- The Shardlands: the bake made the ice spires pale and milky (the deep
  blue Gary asked for on 6 November lost), whether the spires wore the
  bake or not (its light probes reach them; a probe-less bake would leave
  only the snow's bounce, which the sky's light already gives), and the
  snow barely changed. Off, not shipped (16 MB). The night's bake came out
  with black and red blots; the tear's light at nothing in the bake is the
  likely cause (`bake_shards.gd` leaves it out now; not re-baked).
- A Shardlands state takes about half an hour to bake here (the editor
  draws the set at every step). Switching the editor's 3D views off to
  save that time seemed to break a bake, but the blots came back without
  it too, so that wasn't proven; the plugin draws them as usual.
