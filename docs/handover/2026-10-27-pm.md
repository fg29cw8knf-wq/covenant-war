## Notes for whoever is next

- A new `class_name` needs `godot --headless --import` before scripts that
  use it will parse in a headless run (DuelSkills, DuelBarks, DuelPuzzles,
  PuzzleMenu came in this session).
- `DuelSkills` and `DuelPuzzles` reach the Game autoload through
  `get_node("/root/Game")`: the rules engine is compiled before the autoloads
  exist in a headless test, and naming `Game` there broke every test.
- Nothing may emit `state_changed` in the middle of an action (a Summon has a
  card in the air): the comeback card's first version did, and `duel_sim`'s
  card counter saw a card missing for a moment.
- Under xvfb a duel draws a frame every 0.4 s or so and tweens fall behind the
  timers: the stills test waits longer than a phone would.
- A riddle's preview shows the damage only (`preview_plain`): saying WINS
  would give the answer away.
