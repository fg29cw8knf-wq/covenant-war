character models), then `docs/handover/2026-10-25.md`. Gary is

## What landed

Gary sent the new Nell (`Nell_model.glb`, 5.3 MB, with three turnaround
pictures): her new look from the Cast tab of the "Still to Make" doc, Nell
Hayes, a Black woman of about seventy, small and a little stooped, silver
hair under a faded blue headscarf, a cream knitted shawl over a plum wool
dress, a carved walking stick in her right hand. It's an AssetFurnace
21-joint rig (no fingers, bones numbered), 0.9 m tall as made, one mesh of
about 20,000 triangles with albedo, normal and metallic-roughness textures,
and 22 clips retargeted from a library (the player's 21 and a
`get_out_of_bed`).

- `tools/rig_villager.sh nell <Nell_model.glb>` builds her: 1.50 m, her
  own 22 clips plus the game's eight fitted from `clips/shared.glb` and
  Bram's lift; 72 inside-out triangles turned round; the stick (744
  vertices, 0.90 m, 7 cm across) found and bound to her right hand alone
  by `stick_weights.py`, as before.
- **The stick pointed back through her skirt** in her first build. The
  library's clips hang her right hand loose at her side, and the stick is
  rigid to that hand, so in her idle and talk it vanished into the dress
  and in her walk it poked out behind her; only the rest pose had her hand
  on it. The 18 October model's own idle had her hand on the stick, which
  is what `--hold-arm Right:idle:...` relied on. `rig_character.py` now
  takes `rest` as the clip to hold an arm from (`--hold-arm Right:rest:...`:
  the arm as in the sculpted rest pose), and Nell holds her right arm so in
  her idle, walk, run, sneak, talk, cheer, dance, look_up, duel_idle,
  play_card, victory, defeat and lift. Her wave still lifts the stick (it
  reads as her waving it, as on 23 October) and her fall takes it with her.
- Her portrait was re-rendered (`assets/art/characters/nell_portrait.png`).
- README: the character-model paragraphs; the "Still to Make" doc: Nell
  taken off the two "Nell and Hob made again" items (Hob and Ned remain)
  and ticked on the Done tab under 6.
