## Things worth knowing

- **`SSS_STRENGTH` in a shader of our own hung the picture** on the test
  machine's Vulkan (lavapipe) the first frame it was drawn, every thread
  waiting; the engine's own `StandardMaterial3D` with subsurface scattering
  didn't. The skin uses `diffuse_lambert_wrap` and `BACKLIGHT` instead. Not
  known whether a Mac or iPhone would have hung too; safer without.
- **`bake_mesh_from_current_skeleton_pose` refuses these models** ("must have
  its skin registered with a valid skeleton", with both there):
  `held_child_probe.skinned` skins them on the CPU instead, read inside
  `skeleton_updated` so every reach and bend is in it.
- **Bram's left shoulder joint is 11 cm behind his middle and the front of
  his coat 13 cm ahead of it**; his arm reaches 50 cm. `held_child_probe ...
  front` prints his front; `... scan` tries places for the bundle.
- **The reach puts the forearm's line 7.5 cm past the wrist on its mark**,
  so a hand's mark under something is a little past where the palm should
  be (`TUCK_HAND`).
- **`white_sky_look ... out/hand` and anything else drawing the hand** hung
  until the shader was fixed: if a drawn run of the walk out stands still
  at about 5 s with no CPU, it's a shader the driver can't take.
- **The yellow at his wrist** in the attic is part of his model (a cuff), not
  the mark.
