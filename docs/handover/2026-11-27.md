model it replaces carried across). `maps_follow_paint.py` gained `--eye`
(grey hair fools the eye finder), wet eyes for an eye the maker painted, a

through `rig_person.sh` the same way; a model whose face or hands differ
gets its `Figure3D` entries (`MOUTHS`, `POSTURE`, `FROZEN`) looked at again,
and any scene that holds things against it (`held_child_probe`, the body
check) run.
