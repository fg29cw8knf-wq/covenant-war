# Handover, 12 November 2026 (evening): the body check, and the first sweep of the Prologue

Read `README.md` first: "The body check (12 November)" (Running the tests)
says what it is and how to run it. Gary is non-technical: plain, short
English. Mac and iPhone builds only (not the phone link).

## What Gary asked

- "we seem to have a lot of model glitches that I'm having to keep go through
  and ask you to fix ... is that a Godot issue or would we still have the same
  issue with unity": the answer was no (the same glitches would come in Unity:
  the models' rigs, borrowed clips on other bodies, close actions staged in
  code), and the offer he took: an automatic check, before every hand-over,
  for hands and bodies going through things and for twisted joints, run over
  the whole Prologue as every look, after the other session's fixes of the
  day were in.
- His new standing rule (also in project memory): every fix or addition is
  looked at for how it could look, feel and play better, and the ideas are
  given to him as suggestions after each build (README, the standing rule).

## What was built

- `tests/body_check.gd`: the check (joint, skin, through, person, hangs), its
  stills (game, close, side), `BODY:` / `BODY-END:` / `BODY SUMMARY` lines.
- `tests/body_probe.tscn`: every model through its clips, the same checks on
  the clips themselves. Every model's rest pose comes out with no bad
  triangles (the CPU skinning matches the renderer's).
- `tests/elbow_probe.tscn`: reaches with the IK pole free and on the elbow's
  hinge (`Figure3D.pole_hinge`, see below).
- `tools/body_sweep.sh` and `tools/body_report.py`: the sweep (snow, P1,
  after midnight, the Thornwood; look A drawn and checked for everyone, B, C
  and D for the player) and its report and contact sheets.
- `tests/draw_hold.gd`: the drawing switch in `keys` mode shared by the
  play-throughs' stills and the check's.
- `DollArt.test_look`: `look=<b|c|d>` on `p1_v7_test` and `film_test` plays the
  player as that look from the start (the game plays Player A).
- `p1_v7_test` takes `qa`, `body` and `look=`; `film_test`, `white_sky_test`
  (and `keys`), `notes_1106_shot` and `after_midnight_shot` take `body`; the
  last two take `hinge`. Headless, `p1_v7_test` never gets past the attic's
  mirror, and drawn it's half an hour a look, so the sweep runs B, C and D
  up to the street there (the attic, the mirror, the porridge, the crate).

## Running it

A drawn run of P1 takes about 30 minutes (the stills at every line are most of
it); after midnight about 17, the Thornwood 9, the snow 4. Drawing every
frame small instead of in `keys` mode is slower, not quicker (a frame of the
green costs a second or two at any size here). Headless, after
midnight and the Thornwood take under a minute. Three Godots at once on this
machine ran out of memory (a run killed, exit 137): one or two at a time.
