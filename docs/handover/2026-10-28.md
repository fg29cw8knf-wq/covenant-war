## What was built

All in `scripts/duel/duel_synergy.gd` (data and words) and `DuelGame`'s
"synergy (28 October)" section; README has the full list. In short:

- Families (7, across elements), each with a Kinship when two stand
  together; Pack Howl and Call of Kin (new Rites, stand-in paintings).
- Side by side (+10 each, same element, neighbouring slots); Shelter, Rally
  and Tend keywords.
- Ten reactions (Melt, Shatter, Steam, Flare, Conduct, Freeze, Overgrow,
  Firestorm, Rend, Nightmare), on attacks and damaging Rites.
- Four new bonds (Flood: River Hound and Brookfin); a Totem can be in two.
- The chain: the third card a turn costs 1 less; Combo cards do more as
  the third or later.
- Every card answers to a god (printed at its foot). Two lesser gods are
  new: Ellowen, the Lantern Keeper (spirit; Ashford's festival) and Orrun,
  the Stone Warden (earth). Each Circle is a god's ground; gods have rivals.
  Graced (its god's ground, or its god Pleased with you: +10 for a Totem,
  1 less for any other card) and Shunned (the rival's ground, or Displeased:
  the reverse).

On the screen: tags on the plaques (the Kinship, SIDE, GRACED/SHUNNED, BOND),
a ribbon on Graced/Shunned hand cards and the real cost in the hand (it
showed the printed cost for Rites and Wards before, Laws included), whose
ground it is in the corner, the chain on the turn line, the reaction named
on the aiming chip and in a banner, the parts of a blow floating over it,
the family on the card face after BASIC TOTEM, the god at the foot. The
Codex gives the family, every bond and the god (with how it feels about you).

A rule seen in a duel with nobody to teach it is kept as `seen:<id>`
(`DuelScreen._seen`): it opens its How to Duel chapter without stopping
the teacher's own lesson later. (The first version marked the lesson itself
and the teacher then never spoke: fixed, and the stills show Tam's line.)
