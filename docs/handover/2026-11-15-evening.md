## Things that are mine, not his (he can change any)

- **The side of Bram's face is seen** in the shot of him and the child. From
  behind or above, his shoulders hid the child completely (seven camera
  places tried and looked at). He allowed the side of the face for the walk
  out; this uses the same allowance. Never from the front.
- **The hunter burns away**; whether that's its end isn't said. Its ash on the
  rim on the walk out.
- **Which side of the neck**: his right. Asleep he lies on his back with his
  head to the west and his left side to the wall, so a mark on the left
  couldn't be filmed in the attic. His own films (wl_03, her_10) will want the
  same side; the film plan says "the side of his neck".
- **"But my chest was on fire."** became "But the mark burned like fire." (his
  line on the bed's edge the morning after).
- **The card that lands**: the gold one of the night's Sigils, the Gilded
  Sentinel's painting. And the white **goes out of it again** as it goes into
  his coat ("for a moment", as the choice he picked said).
- **The finale's shots** (the flank wide with Orlanthe looming on the right,
  the hunter close on a long lens, the last one behind Bram), its length
  (about 7 s), Orlanthe leaving up the light.
- **The title's painting drifts in** a little while it's up.

## Things worth knowing

- **The duel played alone (`white_sky_test ... duel`/`page`) never had the
  child with Bram**: it was still lying in the crater in the walk's set. The
  shot down at the child looked at the snow 30 m off. The test now puts the
  child straight into his coat (`_carry`) and `_to_the_child` skips the shot
  if the child isn't with him.
- **The baby lies under his bowed head between his shoulders and his left
  arm**: there's no clear line to it from behind or above. That's why the
  side shot.
- **A shattered Totem hides its own model** (`DuelField3D.sync`: `not
  s.shattered`), so `crystal_death` on the hunter's pack outside the duel's
  rules is safe; the one the duel itself knocks out is left to the screen
  (`_wipe_one` checks the game's Totem).
- **The hunter's node is left up after it burns** (its meshes hidden by
  `burn_away`) so its ash drifts on; `_walk_out` hides it.
- **Untyped `for i in f.slots[1].size()`**: `i` is a Variant; a `:=` from it
  failed to compile (`delay`).
- **The neck mark sat under the collar** at first (the collar stands up at the
  side of the neck); `mark_neck_look ... xray` draws it through what's over
  it to find it. The Neck bone is near the top of the neck on these models
  (the Head bone only 5 to 7.5 cm above it).
- **Headless runs' game time includes the Summon's video by the wall's
  clock** (about 950 s of game time in `white_sky_test` whole headless): not
  new.
- **`white_sky_test ... body`** finds Bram's hands in the bundle as he lifts
  and carries it (the pick-up and the walk out): the same kind as before,
  not from this.
