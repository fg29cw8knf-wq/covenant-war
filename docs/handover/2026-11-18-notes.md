## Things worth knowing

- **Read from his note 5** as the title that comes up after Bram's memory
  ("the start of game covenant war"): it stands in his list between the
  walk out and the attic. If he meant the title screen, the same lettering
  can go there (`TitleScreen.Logo`).
- **Note 3 wasn't reproduced.** Headless, no frame of the walk out takes
  over 20 ms with the warm-up or without it. What a Mac spends compiling a
  shader the first time it's drawn can't be seen here. If it still waits,
  he's been asked what he sees (a white screen, or the picture stood still)
  and for how long.
- **Note 10 wasn't found, but it's the picture, not the game's own work.**
  Nothing here can measure a frame rate. What can be measured is what a
  frame costs the processor with nothing drawn (`frame_cost_probe`): 1.0 ms
  on the evening green, 0.9 ms in the afternoon, 0.2 ms in the snow, on
  this machine's two slow cores. So 15 to 19 frames a second is the
  graphics card's share. The evening green as a Mac builds it: 41 lights
  lit (one with shadows, the moon), 17 particle systems (364 particles),
  bounce light (SDFGI, six cascades, 15 cm cells), SSIL, SSAO, volumetric
  fog, glow, 2x MSAA, at the screen's full size. The guard's changes are
  sure to help a Mac that needs the smallest size (it gets there in 9 s,
  and starts there next time). If "Best picture" is on, 15 to 19 is what
  it costs on an M1, and the game now says so. He's been asked: Mac or
  iPhone, where in the game, and what the meter says after the number.
  Offered to him if it's still slow: a "Smooth" picture that leaves out
  the costliest lighting. Still waiting for his yes from 12 November: the
  textures stored the way graphics cards read them (a quarter of the
  memory).
- **A material's `emission` is added to its `emission_texture`** unless
  `emission_operator` is MULTIPLY. A white picture with black emission
  shines white. Worth a look anywhere else a light is "put out" by setting
  it black.
- **`(far[part] as PackedFloat32Array).append(x)` appends to a copy.** A
  packed array kept in an Array and cast is a value: use an Array of
  Arrays, or a variable of its own.
- **A reach's mark is where the forearm's line ends, 7.5 cm past the
  wrist; a turned hand's palm is somewhere else.** With his fingers turned
  across (the take), his palm's middle was 4.5 cm under the mark:
  `TAKE_AT` has that taken off. `take_palm_at` says where it really was.
- **A test's still takes three frames**, and at ten frames a second the
  moment has gone. `held_child_probe._shot` all but stops the game
  (`Engine.time_scale` 0.001) while it draws.
- **Headless, a tap in the first 300 ms by the clock after a thing's
  offered isn't taken** (`AshfordP1.Use._armed`): the game's half second
  goes by in milliseconds. `eat_probe` and `moth_touch_probe` wait it out;
  `p1_v7_test` needs drawing (`keys`).
- **`Figure3D.pose("lift", 0.42)` isn't a kneel**: the shoulders stay 1.3 m
  up. `kneel(secs, lean, false, true)` is the one-knee kneel at its right
  height.
- **The attic's blanket is a stiff sheet** (a grid of points laid high over
  him). It reads as a board in the whole-of-him shot. Suggested to him,
  below.
