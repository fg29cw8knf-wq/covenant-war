## Things worth knowing

- **The guard's kept sizes are in the Options** (`gfx_places`: place ->
  [step, ceiling, the day]; and "as": what the picture was being drawn as).
  Deleting the key finds them afresh. `gfx_step` is still kept, as the size
  the session starts at.
- **A place the game never names stays whatever was named last.** After the
  Prologue that's "ashford morning: the green" for the rest of Ashford. Name
  more places (`PerfLog.at`) as they matter: the evening, midnight.
- **`PerfLog.at` is also the guard's place.** A new heavy scene that should
  have its own size needs naming once.
- **`_slow_now` takes the clock over** from a slow motion that's running
  (`_slow_id`); `_slow` still stands aside if one is. The test fails if the
  clock is left under 1 at the end.
- **`DuelScreen.strike_lands` is sent for every attack's blow on a Totem**,
  film or not; `strike_goes` only for a film's staged blow.
- **`Slot.lunge` is added to where a Totem is drawn** (model, cut-out,
  shadow, trail, marks), and `foot_world`/`body_world` follow it. Nothing but
  `DuelField3D.lunge` sets it.
- **Played duels are untouched**: the staging is behind `spec.film_strikes`.
  Offered to Gary for every duel (suggestion 2).
- **A changed line loses its recording**: none changed today. Morrowen's
  five lines are still to record (0002 to 0006); none of hers in the game is
  recorded (the one file on disk, `6ec01c26`, is the old "the debt came
  due").

## All seven

1. **Lighting.** The blows' own light (the charge's glow, the flash where
   one lands) is what a played duel has, now in the film too. No place's
   light changed.
2. **Materials.** The shield's rim over the bar: dark rough bronze for bright
   copper. The mark's patch wears the mark's own material.
3. **Animation.** The Totems' leap (gather, spring, stretch, land, back);
   the caster's lean into its blow; his hands coming up to the crate; the
   lid lifted; the card brought down against his chest.
4. **Camera.** Three shots a blow (close as it gathers with a push in, the
   two of them as it crosses with the camera going in, the cut as it lands);
   cuts for sweeps; wide after a knockout; the box's shot from the box's
   side.
5. **Effects.** The charge (motes drawn in, the glow, the sigil under it),
   the trail off a leaping Totem, the crossing's slow motion and its grade.
6. **Colour grade.** The slow-motion grade on the crossing, as the blows'
   freeze has.
7. **Consistency.** The film's blows look as a played duel's do.
