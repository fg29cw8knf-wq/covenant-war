## Things that are mine, not his (he can change any)

- **All the new words.** Morrowen's three lines and the two changed Bram lines
  are mine; he'll want to hear them. Kept as they were: "Not yet. Not yet.",
  "One more hour. That's all I need." (it's the Unspent Hour's line, and only
  makes sense if you know the card's name: offered to him), "Get back from
  him.", "You'll not have him.", "Write it in my name, then.", "Come on then,
  Sprout.", "Not this one."
- **"Near dawn"** for the caption's first line.
- **The hooded figure comes back after burning.** It burned in the Last Page's
  light (his 15 November ask) and now it's seen whole again on the rim as he
  walks off, and goes in the gust by burning again, in a cold light (its
  embers are the warm ones of the duel's burn).
- **The small arm's place**: the child's outer shoulder, 4 cm towards the head
  from the bundle's middle, on its top; at least 57 degrees off flat.
- **The pace**: every number in the README's list. The duel's own end isn't
  slower: it's timed to Gary's end music (`white_sky_end`).
- **The bed**: no video could be watched from here. The clip (Hob's, Tripo3D)
  was kept and paced against two studies (a 2023 Scientific Reports study of
  young adults getting up from lying, s41598-023-38779-2: lying, onto an
  elbow, then a hand, then sitting; Abdolrahmani and Yonetsu 2016, sit to
  stand 3.3 s in fifteen young adults). Offered to him: film himself (or a
  stock clip) getting out of a bed and turn it into a motion file with
  Rokoko Vision (free, 30 s a month, FBX with a Mixamo skeleton), which can
  be put on Player A as `get_out_of_bed`.

## Things worth knowing

- **A changed line loses its recording.** The game finds a recording by the
  words' id; the old files for "Hush, now. I've got you." (`b3db9878`),
  "I've got you. I've got you." (`81601230`) and "Twenty years later, the
  debt came due." (`6ec01c26`) are still on disk and no longer used. Until
  the new ones are recorded the new lines show as text: Morrowen's wait for
  a tap; Bram's in the duel go by themselves.
- **`white_sky_look ... out` didn't tap lines on the walk out**; it does now
  (the walk out has a line that waits). Before that, a run with a line
  waiting ran to its 90 s and closed under it: the white screen with her
  line on it in that run's `end_1_line` is the test's, not the game's.
- **`hunter_gone` goes false and true again** on the walk out now (true from
  the duel's end, false while it watches, true after the gust). `far_2_going`
  and `far_3_gone` in `white_sky_look` fire on it, so they're taken at the
  start of the walk out as well.
- **`Engine.time_scale` is 0.55 through the three flashes** (`_flashes`); it's
  put back after them, and `_close` puts it back if skipped mid-way. The
  slow-motion grade (`DuelField3D.slow_grade`) is on for them too.
- **`AnimationPlayer.speed_scale` is set every frame during the get-up**
  (`_rise_from_bed`'s `warp`) and put back to 1.0 after it.
- **`bed_check` follows the dream's way, not the memory's**: its "game" stills
  are black through the rise. `mark_wake_test` is the one that follows the
  game's way into the attic.
