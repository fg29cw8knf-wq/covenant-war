On 18 October one session ran eleven parallel agents for about 14 hours and used
38% of Gary's weekly allowance. He was not happy. **Ask Gary before starting
anything large**, say roughly how big it is, prefer one or two agents at a
time, and keep each agent's checks lean (render only what changed). Watch for
agents stalled on approval prompts: an Artifact republish and a complex shell
command each sat for 4-6 hours waiting for an approval nobody saw. This session
was attached to Gary's "card game" Project, which may be why it came out of his
weekly allowance rather than his cloud-session credit; nobody has confirmed how
that billing works.
