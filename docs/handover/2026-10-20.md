For a fresh Claude session on this repo. Gary owns the game and is non-technical:
write to him in plain, short English. Read `README.md` first, then

## What Gary reported from playing the opening, and what was done

He played the opening (on 20 October, the build he had) and listed: lights
floating about in the attic and the taproom; "Thought you'd sleep through the
end of the world" not making sense; the stairs letting him walk through them
with no word from Bram, and Bram walking through the table; "If you need to
stop, stop" wanting a better line; a long wait after skipping the movie; the
player halfway through the bed and an unnatural get-up; and what "I dreamt
about the sky again" refers to and whether the dream needs showing.

Some of these (the solid stairs, Bram round the table, the bed) were done on 19
October and published at 00:44 that night; he may have been on an older build
(the Xcode app has to be downloaded again; the phone link's Home Screen app can
hold an old version until it's reloaded). What was actually wrong on main, and
what changed:

- **Floating lights**: dust-mote particles in all four inn rooms (`_motes` in
  `inn_rooms.gd`), bright 1 cm sparks that read as lights on a phone. Removed.
- **Stairs**: Bram's "Wrong way" line never fired: `walk_room` read a
  `stray_zone` key and the taproom supplied `stairs_zone`. Fixed, and the player
  is now stopped and turned back a step while he says it (`held_until` in
  `walk_room`). The stairs were already solid (`keep_out`). Bram's follow was
  already clamped round the table (`_follow_step` with the room).
  `tests/ashford_test.gd` (opening part) now taps towards the stairs and checks
  the line and the player's spot.
- **Lines**: "There you are. Last night of the Age, and you'd have slept through
  it." and "And if you want a breather, take one. Even the end of an Age can
  wait a minute." (`TAPROOM`, `AT_DOOR` in `ashford_p1.gd`; noted in
  `docs/script/v3_prologue_p1.md`; not yet in Gary's master doc).
- **The dream**: it was a flat pale grey blooming to white (it looked like a
  broken screen). Now the night sky from the bed, stars, and a white light
  climbing from the bottom until nothing's left but white: the Sigilfall before
  it happens (`CineOverlay.dream_sky()`, 3.4 s, `AshfordP1.DREAM_SECS`; 0 turns
  it off). Gary was told what it's for; he may still want it out.
- **The bed**: the sleeper lay with his head inside the bolster and his boots
  up through the blanket. Now a flatter pillow, the body tipped up 13 degrees at
  the head onto it, feet dropped 55 degrees, the blanket raised over the boots,
  hips 0.14 m nearer the head (`Figure3D.LIE_HEAD_UP`, `LIE_FOOT_DROP`,
  `InnRooms.PILLOW_FLAT`, `lie_at`). The get-up is slower and he pushes up on a
  hand on the mattress (`reach`), sits a beat on the edge, then stands.
  `tests/bed_check.tscn` renders it from the side and the foot at given seconds.
  A real wake-up still wants a motion-captured clip; Gary's been asked for one
  (AssetFurnace, or Mixamo's "Waking Up"/"Getting Up", which the rig tool can
  borrow if the bone names match the shared set).
- **The wait after Skip**: on the web the whole movie pack downloaded before the
  Skip button existed. The loading screen now has Skip (`Packs.need("movie",
  true)` returns false; the fetch is aborted). If the movie ends before Ashford
  is prepared, `Packs.hold("Lighting the lanterns in Ashford")` shows a creeping
  bar instead of black. Not yet timed on a phone.
