# Handover, 14 November 2026: the Shardlands' look, a smoother walk, and a more dramatic duel

Read `README.md` first: "The Shardlands' look and the duel's drama (14
November)" (Story mode) has what was asked and what each part became. Gary
is non-technical: plain, short English. Mac and iPhone builds only.

Gary (14 November): "Can we make the bram bit at the beginning look visually
better and smoother - I want best looking graphics and scenery - also how
could we make the battle more dramatic".

**There were two passes that day.** The first is below. The second, "Gary's
eight yeses" (the knee, the phone's shake, the opening's wide shot, his
coat in the wind, the gusts, the hunter's arrival, the ice, its sounds), is
the second half of this file and the README's "The Shardlands, second pass".

One session, one worker. No graphics card here: Forward+ through lavapipe
(`VK_ICD_FILENAMES=/opt/lavapipe/lvp_icd.json`, `--rendering-driver vulkan
--rendering-method forward_plus`), every run under `flock /tmp/godot.lock`.
A full `white_sky_test ... keys` takes about 25 minutes, the duel alone
(`duel`) eleven, `tests/white_sky_look.tscn` (new) two to eight.

## What was wrong, seen in stills before anything was changed

- The walk was one pale lilac wash: the volumetric fog lit the whole sky
  (its `sky_affect` is 1 unless told), so it was never night. The snow was a
  level floor with dots. The flakes were hard round spots. The "sheets of
  snow along the ground" stood upright like rain: a particle billboard
  ignores the way the particle is going. Nothing stood on the horizon. The
  crater's light couldn't be seen until he stood on its rim, and in the
  bowl its fog burned every close shot to white.
- The duel stood on a different set: a flat dark blue floor that read as
  sea, a black sky, a few crystals. And a film has none of an ordinary
  duel's drama (no freeze, no slow motion, no punch: `DuelScreen._filmed`).
- The wide shot before the hunter comes was taken from under the bowl's
  lip, through the back of the snow.

## Things worth knowing

- `Color` values given to `source_color` uniforms and to `Environment` are
  sRGB. The first far shards came out black for that reason.
- The crater's column and glow (`_shaft`, `_halo`) are billboards standing
  at the crater's middle. From the rim or in the bowl they'd lie across the
  whole picture, so they sink as he comes near (`_process`, by
  `_crater_dist()`) and are gone by the rim's shot.
- `_slow` times itself by frames' own length (the frame's delta over the
  clock's scale), not the wall clock: a headless test runs thousands of
  frames a second, and by the wall clock the "slow motion" would have run on
  for half a minute of game time.
- A material's `get_shader_parameter` gives null for a parameter never set
  (not the shader's default).
- The snow's ridges read as water if the light lies too low across them or
  they're too strong: the duel's field has `relief` 0.32 and its light at 19
  degrees for that.
- Nothing here changes what happens in the duel, its order, or how long a
  player has: `white_sky_play_probe` is untouched and passes.

## Not done, and why

- **Not seen on a real Mac or iPhone.** Everything was looked at in stills
  from a software renderer. The frame rate can't be measured here. The
  snow's shader is lighter than it was (a dozen texture reads where it
  worked eleven layers of noise out per pixel) and nothing heavy was added
  to the walk; the duel gained the walk's fog and storm on a Mac. If the
  duel runs slower on his M1, the first thing to take off is
  `volumetric_fog_enabled` in `_dress_duel`.
- **The phone's Smooth picture** (`-- --phone`) was looked at for the walk
  and the rim only (`white_sky_look`), not the duel. The phone link's
  (Compatibility) was not rendered at all: it isn't built any more.
- **The hunter has no gesture of its own** (its model has idle and walk
  only): it comes a pace nearer and raises its cards.
- The body sweep (`tools/body_sweep.sh snow`) was not run: no pose of his
  was changed, only where the cameras and lights are.

## Things worth knowing

- **Nothing takes the walk from him** (his rule of 5 November) still holds
  with the wide shot: `_roam` is on from the frame the black begins to
  lift, and steering hurries the camera in (`OPEN_HURRY`). The test plays it
  both ways (`hurry`).
- **Where the wide shot stands was searched for**, not guessed: the spires
  are laid by a seeded generator, and two tries had a great shard in the
  middle of the picture or walling it in. If `_build_spires` or its seed
  changes, look at `white_sky_look ... open` again.
- **Morrowen's opening line waits** for the camera to be half way down
  (`OPEN_LINE_AT`): its subtitle lay over him in the wide shot.
- **Gusts start from his first steps**, not from the tear: the walk to the
  rim is about twenty metres, and a gust that waited for the sky to open
  never came. None during the glimpse's hush; one as the storm comes back
  after it.
- **Bram's mesh is rebuilt once** for this memory (`ShardSet._weather_mesh`:
  the same arrays with vertex colours added, 38 thousand vertices, kept for
  the session) and he wears `WEATHER` in place of his model's
  StandardMaterial3D, here only. His model elsewhere is untouched. The mask
  is measured from where his skin says his bones are, so another long-coated
  model should work, but only Bram's has been looked at.
- **His hair is a tied-back knot** in the model: it stirs, a couple of
  centimetres. Loose hair in the wind would need a model with it.
- **The child is carried by his chest bone** from his second Totem's fall
  (`_carry_child`); before that it's at a fixed place in his own frame as
  it always was.
- **`HEAD_KNELT` is measured** (the test prints where his head is on his
  knee and fails if the constant is more than 22 cm off). If the kneel's
  lean (`KNEE_LEAN`) changes, read the new number off the test.
- **Haptics can't be felt here.** `Haptics.count` and `.last` say what was
  asked for; whether an iPhone buzzes, and whether the strengths feel
  right, is for Gary's hand.
- **The ice's sounds are synthesised stand-ins** (22 kHz, made by
  `Sfx._synth` the first time they're asked for, which WhiteSky does under
  the opening's black). Gary's files replace them by name.
- **A new `class_name` (`Haptics`)**: a local checkout needs one
  `godot --headless --import` before tests will find it (the builds import
  anyway).

# The third pass, that evening: "1 yes 4 yes" again

His answer to the second pass's suggestions: the phone's shake in every
duel, and a "Best picture" switch on the Mac. The README's "The phone's
shake in every duel, and "Best picture" on a Mac" has both in full.

## What was built

- **The phone shakes in every duel** (`DuelScreen._buzz`): a big blow on a
  Totem or a duellist, a Totem broken, the blow that ends it. Not small
  blows, not a duel run at a watcher's speed, not the filmed duel (which
  has its own). The Vibration switch turns it all off.
- **"Best picture"** (`Gfx.Best`; Options on a Mac or PC, off by default):
  4x MSAA where a view had 2x, shadow maps twice as fine with the softer
  filter, finer SSAO, 16x anisotropic filtering, full-detail models,
  debanding. The frame-rate guard stands down while it's on.

## Things worth knowing

- **Best picture is off by default on purpose.** Gary asked for the best
  possible graphics, but he also reported the whole game running slow on
  11 November, and what this costs on his M1 can't be measured here. He was
  told where the switch is and to say if it stutters. If he reports it
  runs well, making it the default on Apple silicon is a one-line change
  (`Gfx.Best.on`'s fallback).
- **The guard and Best picture can't both act.** `Gfx.Guard.on()` is false
  while Best picture is on, whatever "Keep the frame rate smooth" says; the
  Options page still shows that switch as he left it.
- **Each view keeps what it was made with** in a meta (`gfx_was`), written
  the first time `Gfx.Best.dress` sees it; switching off restores from it.
  A view that changes its own MSAA or lamp shadow size *after* `Gfx.apply`
  would be put back to the earlier value by a later switch-off: set them
  before the call (as `DuelField3D` now does).
- **Views that never go through `Gfx.apply`** (the inn's mirror, room
  previews) are left alone.
- **The shadow map at 8192** is 128 MB of the Mac's memory more than at
  4096.
- **The local texture trick**: `/tmp/import_patched.tgz` now keeps the
  patched `.import` files as Godot rewrote them, so after `git checkout`
  they can be put back without a re-import (untar over the tree).

## Checked

- `tests/best_picture_test.tscn` (new) ok, and `-- --phone` ok;
  `tests/haptics_test.tscn` (new) ok.
- Stills of the Shardlands with Best picture off and on
  (`white_sky_look <dir> torn close -- --best`). **The first one was
  wrong**: a row of white blots across the sky. It was a bug of the
  morning's (`ShardSet.SHAFT`: a power of a number under nothing along the

- The difference Best picture makes in these stills is small (this scene

  is on the page on a computer; Vibration on a phone); `load_all` 0 failed.
