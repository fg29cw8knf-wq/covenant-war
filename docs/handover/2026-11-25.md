## Things worth knowing about the staging

- **When it's on:** the cinematic touches on (`DuelRules.cinematic`), a 3D
  field, not a film, and Normal or Fast (`STAGE_MAX_SPEED` 2.0). Fastest, the
  flat field, a film and `staged_strikes = false` keep the attack as it was
  (the push in and the whip), which `duel_cine_shot attack` still checks.
- **Screen geography:** the crossing is always filmed from the player's half
  (`crossing_shot(near, far)`: near is the player's Totem, or where a blow on
  the player's Life lands). So the player's Totems are always the near ones.
- **`crossing_shot` marks every place** (`_cross_mark`, the lower the better)
  and takes the best; `cross_pick` says which and its mark, and
  `duel_cine_shot crossings` renders the pick for every pair of slots on three
  boards. The marks are tuned by eye on stills: if a board turns up that it
  films badly, that's where to look. WhiteSky's own `_crossing_shot` (first
  clear place of four, Bram's face kept behind the lens) is untouched.
- **A cut needs `snap_camera()`** before anything is put on the stage by
  where a Totem is on the screen (`_center`, `world_to_stage`): `cam_shot`
  with `cut` sets `cam_pos`, but the Camera3D only moves in `_process`.
  `fx_attack`, `_deliver_staged` and `_whiff` take their positions after it.
- **The clock:** the crossing's slow motion (`_cross_slow`) holds
  `_time_busy`, and ends itself or is ended as the blow lands
  (`_cross_slow_end`, by `_cross_id`), so the hit-stop on a big blow, which
  needs `_may_slow()`, still comes. Its lengths are the watch's milliseconds,
  shortened at Fast.
- **Between the blows of one attack** `_deliver_staged` bumps
  `field.cam_moves`, or the way home planned after the first blow
  (`_cam_home_soon`) would take the camera away under the second.
- **The finishing blow** (`_finishing_start`) cuts home before its lean when
  the attack was staged; `_deliver_staged` then leaves the camera and the
  clock alone (`free` false) and only runs the leap.
- **Tests' time:** `_real_wait`, `_time_ramp` and `_cross_slow` are timed by
  the watch, which a headless `--fixed-fps` run outruns, so frame counts from
  `duel_monkey` don't measure a duel's length. The cost in time in the README
  (0.7 s an attack at Normal) is worked out from the constants.
- **Two old faults found on the way:** a slot's rim-light colour was set once
  (`_tint_model`; a Totem called into a slot struck in the last one's
  colour), and `_end_charge` used `is` on a freed charge.

  colour warmed towards white, from beside the lens.

  crossing slowed for blows that matter.

  arena, at Normal and Fast.
