# The Spoken Script: Prologue P1 (Ashford, before midnight)

_A copy of section P1 of Gary's "The Spoken Script" Claude doc (https://claude.ai/artifact/2ki6rTJKfqHP3A29RmLXe6), taken on 16 October 2026 so a coding session without the doc can build from it. The doc is the master: if they differ, the doc wins._

### P1 · The attic and the taproom

_Built: after the movie, the camera flies in over Ashford's rooftops, past the unlit Great Lantern, down to the player on the green as Bram walks over, and his lesson plays there. Still to build: the attic, the crate and the lantern hanging._

_Black. The festival drum from the movie keeps beating._

**Caption:** Ashford. The last night of the Age.

_The inn's attic: low beams, a straw bed, a cracked mirror. The player wakes to the drum and looks in the mirror._

**Narration:** Nobody alive has seen an Age end. So Ashford has decided it ends with cider.

**Objective:** Choose your look. (Tap a face.)

_Cut to the taproom. Bram is stacking paper lanterns into a crate._

**Bram:** There you are, Sprout. Thought you'd sleep through the end of the world.

**Bram:** Once every two thousand years, an Age ends. Be a shame to be late for it.

**The player's choice:** What happens when it ends? / I'm up, I'm up.

**Bram (what happens):** The gods fight over who sits the Throne next. We hang lanterns and drink to whoever wins. It's a better deal than it sounds.

**Bram (I'm up):** Good. Crate's heavy. Builds character.

**Bram:** Corin's telling everyone he'll duel at the Sun Court one day. Solhaven. Gold roofs and golden knights.

**The player's choice:** One day I will, too. / Why would anyone want to?

**Bram (one day):** Nobody from Ashford goes to Solhaven, Sprout. Solhaven would have to know where Ashford is first. Carry the crate.

**Bram (why would anyone):** Because you're twenty and this is a small place. Carry the crate.

**Bram:** Here's how it goes. Tap the ground where you want to stand. Your legs know the rest.

**Bram:** And if you need to stop, the little square up in the corner stops the world. Save there. Nobody will think less of you.

**Objective:** Carry the lantern crate to the green. (Tap the ground to walk.)

_Bram follows the player out. A street of thatched cottages and bunting; the green beyond, with stalls, a fiddler, children with paper lanterns and the Great Lantern on its tall pole._

**Bram (if you dawdle):** The green, Sprout. Big open bit with the pole in the middle. Hard to miss, and you're managing.

**Bram (at the green):** Set it down. Three lanterns on the big pole. When something will let you use it, it lights up a touch. Walk to it and tap it.

**Objective:** Hang three lanterns on the Great Lantern's pole. (Walk up to a glowing hook and tap it.)

**Bram (after the first lantern):** That's one. Straighter than Corin's, which isn't saying much.

**Bram (after the third):** Good. Now the hard part. Half the village wants a hand with something, and you're the hand.

**Bram:** Tam's at the hay cart, Tilly's by the stall and Old Nell's at the well. Walk up to someone and a mark shows over their head. Tap Talk, and they'll talk.

**Bram:** Or go and shut Corin up first, if you must. He's by the well, telling people he's lighting the Great Lantern tonight. I'd like him to be wrong in front of a crowd.

**Bram:** Your choice. But folk here remember who turned up.

**Narration:** He glances up at the stars the way he'd watch a stranger's hands.

**Bram:** Go on. Folk are waiting.

**Objective:** Duel Corin by the well. Tam, Tilly and Old Nell could use a hand first. (Tap Talk when you're near someone.)

### P1 · Bram, when you talk to him

**Bram (while villagers still need a hand):** Still folk wanting a hand, Sprout, if you've the time before midnight.

**Bram (if you haven't helped Tam):** Tam's at the hay cart, losing an argument with a barrel.

**Bram (if you haven't seen Tilly):** Tilly's at the stall with something to show you. She'll burst if you don't look.

**Bram (if you haven't sat with Nell):** And Old Nell's at the well. She'll want to tell you a story. Let her.

**Bram (once all three are done):** Everyone sorted? Good. Corin's by the well, warming up his audience. Go and spoil the ending. Leave the how to me. Whoever wins lights the Great Lantern at midnight.

**Bram (always):** And Sprout. Stay where I can see you tonight. Humour an old man.

### P1 · Tam at the hay cart

_Built: you and Tam carry the barrel from the hay cart to Tilly's stall between you, before the narration._

**Tam (first time):** Evening. Take the other end of this, would you? Bram wants the cider at the stall before the dancing starts, and my back wants a word with Bram.

**Objective:** Carry the barrel to the stall with Tam. (Tap the stall.)

**Narration:** You heave the last barrel into place. Tam wipes his hands on his shirt and nods up the lane, towards the mill.

**Tam:** Ironvault's man came by this morning. Very polite about it. The mill's note falls due at the new moon.

**Tam:** Forty years I've ground Ashford's flour, and a banker I've never met owns the stones. Ah, well. Not tonight.

**Tam:** Solmaris kept the rain fair for two thousand years. Who knows what we get tomorrow. Fire, probably. Or frogs. Drink up while it's free.

**Tam (afterwards):** Whatever's owed can stay owed till morning. If you see my Nell, tell her I'm saving her a dance. Knees or no knees.

### P1 · Tilly at the stall

_Built: the moth unfolds out of Tilly's card, flies twice round her head and settles back into it._

**Tilly (first time):** Look! Look, look, look! A Lantern Moth! Da found it in the barley. It's only a Spark. But it's mine.

**Tilly:** Only Attuned can wake them. Corin says you're Attuned. He says he's more Attuned, but he says that about everything. Will you wake it?

**The player's choice:** Let me see it. / Not tonight, Tilly.

**Objective (if you say yes):** Wake the Lantern Moth. (Tap the card.)

**Narration (if you say yes):** You touch the card.

_The moth unfolds from the card and flies twice round Tilly's head (played, no words)._

**Narration (after it settles):** A moth made of lamplight. It circled Tilly's head twice and settled back into the paper, still glowing.

**Tilly (if you say yes):** Again! Again! It never does that for Da. Corin can make it twitch, but it never flies for him.

**Tilly (if you say not tonight):** Aww. That's all right. It glows brighter when you're near, though. Look!

**Narration (if you say not tonight):** The card in her hands brightens as you lean in. Not a flicker. Steady, the way a candle burns when every door is shut.

**Tilly (afterwards, if you woke it):** It's still glowing! It never glows this long for me.

**Tilly (afterwards, if you said not tonight):** You said not tonight. It's still glowing, though. Look!

### P1 · Old Nell at the well

**Nell (first time):** Sit with me a moment, child. My knees won't take the dancing. Don't tell Tam. He'll only try to carry me.

**Objective:** Sit with Nell. (Tap the bench.)

**Nell:** My gran's gran used to say the last Age began with lights in the sky. Gods squabbling over a chair, she said, and the sparks falling on us.

**Nell:** I've seen the sky do it once myself, years back. One light. Left a hole in the top field you could lose a cow in.

**Nell:** Stay close to Bram tonight, child. Old bones feel things.

**Nell (afterwards):** Lights in the sky, child. Mark an old woman's words.

### P1 · Seamus the fiddler and Hob

**Seamus:** Requests? I know the Lantern Reel, the Lantern Reel played slow, and the Lantern Reel played drunk. Two of those I can still play.

**Seamus:** They say this reel's as old as the Age. Somebody stood on this green and played it the last time the sky changed hands. I do wonder how his night went.

**Hob (new; he stands by the barley stall):** Barley's in, roof's patched, cider's paid for. If the Age wants to end, it can end tonight. I'm free.

**Hob:** My cabbages came up early this year. Can't think why.

### P1 · Corin, Bram's deck and the Lantern Duel

_Built (15 October): Corin points out you have no deck, Bram walks over and gives you your first one, shows you round your pack, and then the duel. After it, Tilly comes running with her moth. 16 October: Bram heads over the moment you start talking to Corin, and Tilly's moth passes into your hands on her words "You keep it". Everyone can work a Sigil's magic; only the Attuned can call a Totem out of one._

_As Corin starts talking, Bram gets up off his stool by the Lantern and heads over at a brisk walk, a worn card box in his hand. He stops a few steps off, listening._

**Corin (when you come near):** Oi! Ashford's other Attuned! Ready to lose? Or are you off helping old ladies first?

**Corin:** Either way, I'm here, deciding which side of my face the crowd gets.

**The player's choice:** Let's duel. / Not yet.

**Corin (not yet****; Bram heads back to his stool****):** Go on, then. Hurry up. My audience is getting cold.

**Corin (let's duel):** Lantern Duel. Winner lights the Great Lantern at midnight. Loser carries the drum home.

**Corin:** Only you've no deck, have you? Every Spark in Ashford sits up when you walk past, and Bram's never once let you hold one.

**Corin:** So I win by default. Again. Shame. I'd practised my face.

_Bram steps in beside you, the card box in his hand._

**Bram:** Nobody wins anything by default while I'm stood here.

**Bram:** I've kept these out of your hands since you were small, Sprout. Reckon the last night of the Age is a fair night to stop.

**The player's choice:** Why now? / Mine? To keep?

**Bram (why now):** Because tomorrow's a new Age, and I'd rather you met it with something in your hand.

**Bram (to keep):** To keep. Twelve Sparks from the old box at the inn. I picked every one.

_He holds the box out and you take it. The twelve cards fan out across the screen: YOUR FIRST DECK, Bram's Spark deck. Deck and Codex open up in your pack._

**Bram:** That's a deck. Shuffled, you draw from the top and play what you can.

**Bram:** Anyone can work a Sigil's magic, mind. A Rite, a bit of magic you cast once and then it's gone, answers Tam as quick as it answers you.

**Bram:** But calling a Totem up out of a card, so it stands and fights? That takes the Attuned. In Ashford, that's you, Corin, and an old innkeeper who should know better.

**Corin:** You? Since when?

**Bram:** Since before you could spell it. Which, I'll grant, wasn't long ago.

_Bram walks you round your pack. Each line lights up the one thing to tap and waits until you've tapped it._

**Bram (the pack button lights up):** Before you go losing it: everything you carry lives in your pack. Tap it, up in the corner.

**Bram:** Tap Deck.

**Bram (the deck page):** That's the twelve you'll duel with tonight. Shuffled before every duel, and you draw from the top.

**Bram:** Twelve's a beginner's deck. A proper one is thirty, and you build it yourself from what you find. Not tonight.

**Bram:** Tap any card. It'll tell you everything about itself.

**Bram (the card up close):** What it costs, its element, what every move does. Read before you play. Corin never does.

**Bram:** Close it.

**Bram:** Now the Codex.

**Bram (the Codex page):** Every card you find gets written in here: your own, and any you see played against you. It's short tonight. It won't stay short.

**Bram:** Close your pack. Corin's waiting, and patience was never his Sigil.

**Corin (if you talk to him again with the deck already in hand):** Deck in your hand and everything. Come on, then, before you lose your nerve.

**Corin:** Finally. Half of Ashford's watching. I told the other half to hurry. And no going easy on me. I'd know.

**Bram:** One Totem each. And nobody sets fire to a stall. I'm looking at you, Corin.

**Corin:** That was one stall.

**Bram:** Want me calling your turns from the stool, Sprout? No shame either way. A Totem in a slot, Essence to pay its way, nothing empty between him and your Life.

**The player's choice:** Talk me through it, Bram. / I've watched enough duels.

**Bram (talk me through it):** Sensible. Listen for me.

**Bram (****watched enough****):** Then I'll keep my mouth shut and my eyebrows ready.

**Corin (if you win):** Fine. Fine! You were better. Tonight. You're still second-best in my book. Go on, light the thing. Next year it's mine.

**Corin (if you lose):** Ha! Second-best, like I always say. You nearly had me, though. Nearly. Come on. Best view of the Lantern's next to me.

_Win or lose: Tilly squeezes through the crowd at a run, her Lantern Moth glowing in her cupped hands. She stops in front of you and holds it out. As she says "You keep it", the light passes from her hands into yours, and then the card shows on screen: NEW SIGIL, Tilly's Lantern Moth._

**Tilly (if you woke her moth at the stall):** For luck! You keep it. It glows better for you anyway.

**Tilly (if you didn't):** For luck! It's a Lantern Moth. If it wakes for anyone, it'll wake for you. You keep it.

**Bram:** In your pack with it, Sprout. It'll keep.

### P1 · Bram, during the Lantern Duel (the tutorial)

_Built (14 October) as a step-by-step lesson, if you asked Bram to coach. Every line waits for you: an explanation stays up until you tap, and an instruction lights up the one thing to tap while everything else is locked. The duel is dealt in order, you go first, and it can't be lost._

**Bram (before the first turn):** Right. Those cards are Sigils. The gods' magic is always leaking into the world, a drop at a time, and where it lands it sets hard. Like frost on a window.

**Bram:** Yours are Sparks, the smallest kind. That's the gods on a quiet day, mind. When they fight, it doesn't drip. It pours.

**Bram:** There's a lot to duelling. More than one night's worth. So tonight, just the basics. The rest you'll learn as more cards come your way.

**Bram (pointing at Corin's Life):** Your Totems do the fighting. Bring his Life down to nothing and you've won.

**Bram**** (tap the die; both dice roll big in the middle of the Circle, and you win the roll):** Tap the die to roll for who goes first. Highest starts.

**Bram (your first turn, pointing at your hand):** Along the bottom, that's your hand. Five to start, and you draw one more every turn after this.

**Bram (tap Brookfin):** Brookfin's your Totem: a Spark of river water. Tap it.

**Bram (the card panel lights up on the right):** That panel on the right is the card up close: its cost, its element and what its moves do. Tap any card, yours or Corin's, whenever you want a closer look.

**Bram (tap the slot):** Now tap the glowing slot. That calls it into the circle.

**Bram (pointing at your Essence):** Calling it cost one Essence. That's the blue gems by your Life. You get one more every turn, so spend them. Nobody ever won a duel with savings.

**Bram:** Nobody attacks on the first turn, and a Totem called this turn needs a moment to find its feet. So that's you done.

**Bram (tap End Turn):** Tap End Turn.

**Bram (Corin calls his pup):** There's his Cinderpup. Fire, see the orb? Remember that.

**Bram (your second turn):** You drew a card. One every turn, like I said.

**Bram (tap Brookfin):** Brookfin can fight now. Tap it.

**Bram (tap Splash):** Pick a move. Splash costs nothing. Undertow costs one Essence, hits harder and heals Brookfin a little. Start with Splash.

**Bram (tap the Cinderpup):** Now tap his Cinderpup.

**Bram (the hit lands on its weakness):** See that? Water puts fire out. Hit a Totem's weakness and it hurts a good deal more. The orb on each card tells you its element.

**Bram (pointing at Bulwark):** You've still two Essence. Not every card's a Totem. That Bulwark's a Rite: a bit of magic you cast once, then it's gone.

**Bram (pointing at Bulwark):** Bulwark throws a wall of stone up round one of your own Totems. The next hit it takes, the stone takes first: up to fifty damage of it.

**Bram (pointing at the Cinderpup):** Corin's pup will go for Brookfin on his turn. He never waits. So the wall goes up now, before he swings.

**Bram (tap Bulwark):** Tap Bulwark.

**Bram (tap Brookfin):** Now tap Brookfin, so the stone goes up round it.

**Bram (tap End Turn):** That'll do for this turn. End it.

**Bram (Corin rolls the die):** That's Fate. Some moves roll the die: roll high, hit hard; roll low, nothing. Corin loves those. Corin loves anything that can go wrong loudly.

**Bram (the Bulwark takes the hit):** And your Bulwark took the lot. Told you.

**Bram (your third turn):** His pup's hurting. Undertow will finish it.

**Bram:** Tap Brookfin. / Undertow, this time. / And tap the Cinderpup.

**Bram (the Cinderpup is knocked out):** Out it goes, back into its card. Nobody bleeds inside a circle. That's the law.

**Bram (pointing at Corin's Life):** Nothing stands across from Brookfin now. Next turn it can go straight at Corin's Life.

**Bram (tap End Turn):** End your turn.

**Bram (Corin's Rite gets through):** He's throwing Rites now. That one got through, but Brookfin's tougher than it looks.

**Bram (your fourth turn):** Now. Tap Brookfin. / Undertow. Make it count. / Tap Corin's Life. There's nothing in the way.

### Bram, the first time each thing appears (any duel he's at: the campfire round, Dask's road duel)

The Lantern Duel teaches only what's on the table that night. Everything else Bram explains once, the first time it happens, in the same gated way (the line stays up until you tap). Every line he gives is kept on the pause menu's How to Duel page, in order.

**Bram (the first Fate roll, if he didn't coach the Lantern Duel):** That's a Fate roll. Some moves roll the die: roll high, hit hard; roll low, nothing. The dice don't care who you are.

**Bram (the first condition or boon to land on either side; the game fills in the Totem, the condition, what it does and how long it lasts):** His Cinderpup is Burned now. Takes 20 damage at its owner's Dawn. Two turns, then it's gone.

**Bram (Dask sets his first Ward):** A Ward. He's set it face down, so you can't read it. It waits, and springs by itself when the right thing happens: a Totem called, a Rite cast, a hit coming in. Mind what you do next.

**Bram (you set your first Ward):** Your Ward's down. It springs on its own when its moment comes; you don't have to do a thing.

**Bram (his first Ward springs):** There it goes. A Ward takes the hit meant for him, or turns it back on you. It's spent now. Next time, think what it's waiting for, and don't give it that.

**Bram (the first time you could Ascend; the game fills in the cards):** That Blazehound is an Ascension: Cinderpup, grown up. Tap it, then tap your Cinderpup, and it steps into the same slot, bigger and nastier, with everything it had. Costs Essence, mind, so think about when.

**Bram (the first time a Summon is in your hand; the game fills in the card and its cost):** That Pyraxis is a Summon. Not a Totem: a piece of something far bigger. It costs 7 Essence, does one great thing, and goes back where it came from. Save it for when it counts.

**Bram (the first time the other side calls a Summon):** That was a Summon. Not a Totem: a piece of something far bigger. One great blow, then it's gone, and it costs him a fortune in Essence, so he can't do it often.

_When Bram is the one across the Circle (the practice round at the campfire), these lines speak of his own cards: "That's my Ward, face down. You can't read it, so you can't plan round it…", "A Ward takes the hit meant for me…", "My Cinderpup is Burned now…", "That was my Summon…"._

**Corin (during the duel, in rotation):** Ha! Didn't see that coming, did you? / That's a lucky draw, that's all. / Cinderpup, do something! / Fine. FINE. Good hit.
