# Master Script v3: the Prologue from midnight to dawn over Solhaven

A plain copy of the lines the game's Prologue films are built from, taken from the
"Movie & Prologue" tab of the **Master Script v3** Claude doc (approved and locked by
Gary on 18 October 2026). The doc is the master: if they ever differ, the doc wins.
Story fact changed since: Pyrrhane, the Burning Crown, is a male god (he/him); Solmaris is female.

Where it is in the game: `scripts/world/areas/ashford.gd` (midnight, dawn, the taproom,
the burning, the cellar), `scripts/world/areas/thornwood.gd` (the river, the campfire,
the second morning, the road, the ridge), Dask's duel in `scripts/story/prologue.gd`,
and the duel screen's story pieces (`scripts/duel_ui/story_loss.gd`, `blank_card_slot.gd`).

## How to read the script

**Speaker:** is a line of dialogue; a note in brackets after a name says when it plays and is never shown. _Italics_ are what we see and what the player does. **Objective:** lines appear on screen as the current task, with the button to press in brackets. **The player's choice:** lists what the player can say. `{name}` is the name the player gives. Only Bram says "Sprout". The player is never given a gendered word. Lines marked (V) are voiced. A dialogue box holds two sentences at most; Ashford speaks a light, rural Northern English.

**Losing a story duel:** the opponent's line for winning plays, then Morrowen (V) takes the story back with one line, never the same one twice running: "No. That isn't how it went." / "No. I have it written differently." / "Not like that. Again." / "That isn't what's in my ledger." She speaks once per duel; after a second loss there is only the sound of a page turning back, and two buttons: Again, and Change deck first.

**Sworn wagers in side stories** are played once, and the story follows the result. The swear screen shows the opponent's duel rule and says "A sworn wager. Win or lose, it stands." with Swear now and Not yet. Closing the app mid-duel always resumes the duel; it never counts as a loss.

### Bram, the first time each thing appears (any duel he's at: Dask's road duel, the practice round)

The Lantern Duel teaches only what's on the table that night. Everything else Bram explains once, the first time it happens, in the same gated way, whoever is across the Circle (Dask, the practice round, Hale, Wren). Every line he gives is kept on the pause menu's How to Duel page, in order, with the full element chart.

**Bram (the first Fate roll, if he didn't coach the Lantern Duel):** That's a Fate roll. High, and it hits hard; low, nothing. The die doesn't care who you are.

**Bram (the first condition or boon on either side; the game fills in the Totem, the condition, what it does and how long):** His Cinderpup's Burned now. It takes 20 at its owner's Dawn. Two turns, then it's gone.

**Bram (the other side sets its first Ward):** A Ward. Face down, so you can't read it. It springs by itself when the right thing happens: a Totem called, a Rite cast, a hit coming in. Mind what you give it.

**Bram (you set your first Ward):** Yours is down. It'll spring on its own when its moment comes. Set them early, while they're cheap.

**Bram (the first Ward springs):** There it goes. It's spent now. Next time, think what it's waiting for, and don't give it that.

**Bram (the first hit on a weakness, if he didn't coach the Lantern Duel):** See that? Every element hurts something and fears something. Twelve of them. You'll learn the rest the way I did: by getting hit.

**Bram (the other side's first Guardian):** That's a Guardian. Everything goes through it first. Break it, or go nowhere.

**Bram (the first Divine Gift, either side):** A Gift. A god's favour, bought with kneeling. Nobody gets many.

**Bram (the first card taken from your hand):** She's had a card off you. Don't sit on what you can play now.

**Bram (the first time you could Ascend; the game fills in the cards):** That Blazehound is an Ascension: Cinderpup, grown up. Play it on your Cinderpup and it steps into the same slot, bigger, with everything it had. It costs Essence, so think about when.

**Bram (the first time a Summon is in your hand; the game fills in the card and its cost):** That Pyraxis is a Summon. Not a Totem: a piece of something far bigger. It costs 7 Essence, does one great thing, and goes back where it came from. Save it for when it counts.

**Bram (the first time the other side calls a Summon):** That was a Summon. One great blow, then it's gone, and it cost a fortune in Essence. Nobody can do that often.

_When Bram is across the Circle (the practice round), these lines speak of his own cards: "That's my Ward, face down…", "My Cinderpup's Burned now…", "That was my Summon…"._

### P2 · Midnight: the Great Lantern and the Sigilfall

_A film in the game's own 3D, from midnight to the cellar: watched rather than played, stopping only for choices and your name. It can be picked up again at any caption._

**Narration:** Midnight. The chapel bell counts twelve, and all of Ashford counts with it.

_You walk up with a lit taper and light the Great Lantern. It catches with a roar, and the whole village roars back. Tam is dancing with Nell, badly._

**Corin:** _(Beside you, watching the crowd.)_ It's a good village, this. Don't tell anyone I said so.

**Bram:** Folk think we send them up for the gods. We don't. A lantern's for whoever's out in the dark, so they know which way is home.

**Bram:** Two thousand years. Wonder who'll be sitting up there tomorrow.

**The player's choice:** Does it matter to us? / Whoever it is owes us a better year.

**Bram (does it matter):** It shouldn't. It always seems to.

**Bram (owes us):** _(A short laugh.)_ Write and tell them. See who writes back.

_Hundreds of paper lanterns rise over Ashford. Then they stop, every one, hanging in the air as if the night has taken hold of them. The fiddle trails off. One by one, faces turn up._

**Corin:** Bram. Is it meant to do that?

_The sky splits from one horizon to the other. Light pours down in every colour. For a breath the children cheer. Then a strike hits the mill, and the thatch goes up._

**Nell:** The mill! The mill's on fire!

**Bram:** Inside! Everyone inside! Sprout, the inn. Now!

_Everyone runs for their doors. Corin grabs Tilly and runs her up the lane past Tam's hay cart. You run for the inn; strikes land around you, and one knocks you flat. A blast on the far side of the hay cart throws it over, onto Corin._

**Tilly:** Corin! CORIN!

_You get up, see him, and run back. Bram is already running too._

**Corin:** My leg. It's stuck, I can't, I can't get it —

**Bram (arriving):** Other side. On three. One, two —

_You and Bram take the cart's top edge, one either side of him, and heave. Corin drags himself clear and the cart crashes back down._

**Corin (free, limping):** I'm all right. I'm all right.

_He isn't. His hands are shaking._

_One light falls differently. White. Slow. Silent. It passes Bram. It passes Corin. It stops at you._

_You put out your hand, and it settles there: a card with nothing on it. Every other light in the sky goes out at once. Then a white mark burns itself into the back of your hand._

**Corin:** It came to you.

_He looks down at his own empty hand._

**Corin:** It just came to you.

_Bram crosses to you and closes your fingers over the card. Its glow dies between them. His hand, you notice, is shaking too._

**Bram:** Put that away. Don't show anyone. Not anyone.

### P3 · Dawn: the Pale Herald

**Caption:** Dawn

_Dawn comes anyway. Smoke lies over the hills. The mill is a black shell. Children pick fallen Sparks out of the furrows and squabble over them like conkers._

**Hob:** Lost half the barley and the mill roof. Found nine cards in the cabbages. Can't tell if that's a good trade.

_A mist comes down the road against the wind. In it walks a grey horse that leaves no prints. Its rider's hood is empty, and it carries a staff topped with a pale lantern. The villagers kneel without being told. Bram's hand on your shoulder brings you down with them._

**Bram:** A Pale Herald. Morrowen's own. Head down. Hand in your pocket.

**The Pale Herald (V):** Hear the Covenant.

**The Pale Herald (V):** The Age of Solmaris is ending. By the Covenant, the Turning begins. For one year, any Seat in Veyl may be challenged by anyone.

**The Pale Herald (V):** Solhaven, for Solmaris: Ser Aldric Vane.

_The Herald's lantern shows a knight kneeling in a sun chapel. Then five more faces, a name written under each, too quick to hold: a scarred warlord, a boy on an ice throne, a grinning captain, a mask in the dark, a blindfolded priestess. The camera stays mostly on Ashford's faces._

**The Pale Herald (V):** Glyphmere, for Aldrith: Magister Tobias Crane.

_At the name, Bram's hand tightens on your shoulder._

**The Pale Herald (V):** And one more. Bound to no god. Holding no Seat. Marked by the Covenant itself.

_The mark on your hand blazes straight through your pocket. Every head in Ashford turns. Last of all, slowly, the empty hood._

**Narration (the name screen):** Nobody has told the Herald your name. The empty hood speaks it anyway. What does it say?

**The Pale Herald:** {name}.

**The Pale Herald (V):** The Unsworn.

**The Pale Herald (V):** Present your mark at the Sun Court of Solhaven before the new moon, or forfeit it.

_The Herald rides back into the mist, and the mist goes with it. Nobody speaks. Tilly, uncertain, waves at the place where it was._

**Caption:** That afternoon

_The taproom, shutters closed. Bram turns the blank card over in the lamplight, the way he'd check a coin._

**Bram:** Thirty years with cards, and I never saw one with nothing on it.

**The player's choice:** What does it do? / You know what it is.

**Bram (what does it do):** Nothing I can see. That's what frightens me.

**Bram (you know):** _(He doesn't look up.)_ I know I'm frightened of it. That'll have to do for today.

**Bram:** Solhaven, then. Before the new moon.

**Bram (if you said you'd go one day):** You always wanted the Sun Court. I'd have given a lot for you to want something else.

**Bram (if you said Corin says a lot of things):** You never wanted Solhaven. Seems Solhaven wants you.

**The player's choice:** Come with me. / I can go alone.

**Bram (come with me):** As if you'd get past the end of the lane without me.

**Bram (alone):** No. _(beat)_ No.

**Bram:** We go in the morning. Eat something.

_He hands the card back and doesn't look at it again._

### P4 · That night: Ashford burns

_A film, with one playable stretch: the back gardens._

**Caption:** That night

_Riders come onto the green in black and gold, torches high, and beside them pad hounds made of metal, lantern-light where their eyes should be. At their head, a woman in a plain dark coat with a chain whip coiled at her belt and a small ledger open in her hand. You and Bram watch from the dark of the inn yard._

**Krell:** Good evening. Sabine Krell, for Ironvault. I'll be brief; it's late.

**Krell:** Every note in Ashford fell due at midnight. The mill. Eleven farms. Every roof in this village but one. Under the terms, Ironvault may take possession of all of it, tonight.

**Krell:** Or I can burn the notes. Every one. For a single card.

**Krell:** Where is the Unsworn?

_Silence. Nell looks at the ground. Hob looks at Nell. Nobody points._

_Krell turns a page._

**Krell:** Mr Hayes. The mill, its stones and its house. Eleven hundred crowns. You would be clear.

_(If you told Tam what his letter said.) Tam glances once at the dark of the inn yard, at you. His face doesn't change at all._

_(If you didn't.) Tam's face changes when she says "fell due at midnight". He looks at the inn yard, at you, and you watch him understand, and then you watch him decide._

_He steps forward, cap in his hands._

**Tam:** They left at noon. Took the Solhaven road. Bram and the youngster both.

**Krell:** _(She looks at him for a long moment.)_ No. They didn't.

_She closes the ledger. A Shieldhound moves._

_Full: the hound takes him at the throat, quick and brutal; when the camera comes back from Nell's face, his blood is between the cobbles and his cap lies where he fell. Reduced: the camera cuts to Nell; when it cuts back, Tam is still._

**Nell (just once, the way she'd call him in for supper):** Tam.

**Krell:** Burn the inn. If they're inside, they'll come out.

_Behind the inn's woodpile, Bram pulls you into the dark._

**Bram:** Listen to me. That card won't stay with anyone who takes it. They can't just have it off you.

**Bram:** They need you to stake it in a sworn duel and lose. Or they need you dead.

**The player's choice:** Then I'll duel them. / They killed Tam.

**Bram (I'll duel them):** Them and their dogs? Not tonight. Not while I've breath.

**Bram (they killed Tam):** I saw. _(beat)_ Later. Now we go. Quiet, and stay behind me.

**Objective:** Follow Bram through the gardens. (Tap the ground to move; stay out of the light.)

_Playable: the torches go into the inn's thatch, and the wind takes it to the next roof. Bram leads you through the back gardens, hedge to hedge, while the hunters' lantern beams sweep the lanes. Being seen sends you back to the last hedge; there is no fail screen. A Shieldhound comes along the lane; you both freeze, and it goes by._

**Hunters (as you pass):** Voss! Check the barns! / Nothing here. / Huntmaster wants the innkeeper alive. / Did you hear that?

_The Crooked Lantern is burning. Bram stops at the back door._

**Bram:** My decks are in the cellar. I'm not leaving them to Ironvault.

**The player's choice:** I'm coming with you. / Bram, the roof!

**Bram (coming with you):** Course you are.

**Bram (the roof):** Then don't stand there watching it.

_The cellar. Smoke curls under the door. Bram heaves open an iron-bound chest, sweeps four worn card boxes into a sack, and drags a rug aside: a trapdoor, and below it a tunnel shored with old timber._

**Bram:** Dug this the year I came. Never told you. Never told anyone.

_The riverbank below the hill. You climb out of the tunnel. Above, Ashford burns. On the far bank the villagers huddle together, safe. Corin is with them, soot on his face, holding Tilly's hand. His mam has her arm round Nell._

**Corin (shouting across the water):** Go! I'll see to them!

**The player's choice:** Come with us! / We'll come back for you!

**Corin (come with us):** And leave Mam? And Nell? Somebody's got to stay. _(beat)_ Go!

**Corin (we'll come back):** You'd better. _(beat)_ Go on! Before they see you!

**Hob:** Wait! _(He wades in up to his knees.)_ These came up in my cabbages. Tam said this morning you'd need them more than me.

_He skims a bundle across the shallows: nine Spark cards, still damp, tied with barley straw. They fan out on screen: NINE SPARKS, from Hob's cabbages._

_Nell stands apart from the others, looking back at the mill. She does not wave._

_Up the hill, the roof of the Crooked Lantern falls in. Bram watches until it is finished._

**Bram:** We'll come back, Sprout. I swear it on the Lantern.

### P5 · The Thornwood road

_The Thornwood film: the walk east, the campfire, the practice round, Dask on the road and dawn over the plains. The two duels are played; the rest you watch._

**Caption:** The Thornwood road · east, towards Solhaven

_The first night. A campfire in a clearing. Bram is roasting something that was probably a rabbit. The sack of card boxes sits beside him, unopened._

**Bram:** Tam ground my flour from the day I opened the inn. Never once weighed it in his own favour.

**Bram:** He stood there and lied to that woman's face for you. And I took you out the back.

**Bram:** I'd do it again tomorrow.

**Bram (if you told Tam what his letter said):** You told him what that letter said, didn't you. So he knew exactly what she was offering him.

**Bram (if you didn't):** He didn't know the note was due, did he. He found out from her, and lied for you anyway.

**The player's choice (if you told him):** He knew. He chose. / I should have lied to him.

**Bram (he chose):** He did. Remember that, when you start thinking it was you.

**Bram (should have lied):** No. He'd have wanted to know what he was buying.

**The player's choice (if you didn't):** I should have told him. / I'm glad he had his night.

**Bram (should have told him):** Maybe. You gave him one good evening. That's not nothing.

**Bram (glad):** So am I.

_Bram unties the sack and sets the four boxes in a row on a log._

**Bram:** My old decks. One for every kind of fight I ever had. You'll want your own now; those Sparks won't last you past Solhaven.

**Objective:** Choose your deck. (Tap a box to see it. Tap again to choose.)

_Choosing a deck opens the deck screen for the first time, and Bram walks you round it, one gated line at a time._

**Bram (the deck screen):** Thirty cards. You build it yourself from what you find, and you change it whenever you like.

**Bram (a card up close):** What it costs, what it is, what it does. Read before you play. Corin never did.

**Bram (the Codex):** Every card you own goes in here, and every card anybody plays against you. It's short. It won't stay short.

**Bram (Emberstorm, Cinderpup):** Emberstorm. Goes first and goes hardest. I won a duke's bet with it, and I was sorry by morning.

**Bram (Tidegrove, Brookfin):** Tidegrove. Patient. Mends what the others break, then drowns them. Nobody ever thanked me for a win with it.

**Bram (Ironstone, Pebblit):** Ironstone. Takes a beating and gives it back twice. The one I'd pick, if I'm honest about myself.

**Bram (Veilwild, Dreamfox):** Veilwild. Tricks and dreams. It's won duels it had no business winning. I never quite trusted it.

**Bram (when you choose):** That one. _(He nods slowly.)_ Yes. That suits you.

**Bram:** Lords used to hire me to settle their quarrels, you know. Duel for a field, duel for a dowry, duel for a bridge. Good money.

**The player's choice:** Why did you stop? / Were you any good?

**Bram (why did you stop):** Someone paid me for a job I shouldn't have taken. _(beat)_ Then I stopped.

**Bram (any good):** Good enough that the people I beat still know my name. Not good enough to be proud of it.

_He banks the fire, and doesn't say anything else that night._

**Caption:** The second day

_Morning on the Thornwood road. Bram sets the four boxes back in the sack and keeps one deck out._

**Bram:** Practice round before we walk? I'll go easy.

**The player's choice:** Let's duel. / Let's walk.

**Bram (if you win the practice duel):** Hm. _(He studies the cards on the log a while.)_ Not bad.

**Bram (if you lose it):** I said easy. I never said soft.

**Bram (let's walk):** Suit yourself.

**Caption:** The second night

_The road narrows between thorn trees. The fireflies go out, all at once._

**Bram:** Stay close.

_A wiry figure steps out of the trees: young, a bronze mask over his mouth, a Shieldhound clanking at his heel. His hands aren't steady._

**Dask:** That's far enough. The card. The blank one.

**Bram:** Covenant law, lad. Stake it in a sworn duel, or walk away.

**Dask:** I know the law. _(beat)_ Fine. Sworn duel, Unsworn. Your card against my deck.

**Bram:** That's every card you own.

**Dask:** And that card's worth more than everything I own. Do the sums, old man.

_You swear, and the air closes around the words like a lock: a ring of light round the two of you. Your card against his deck._

**DUEL · The Road** · Dask · Ironvault Hounds. _Duel rule: Run to Ground. Dask's hounds always attack the Totem with the least Life left. Bram coaches from the roadside, one line as each new thing first appears._

**Dask (his first Fate roll):** Come on. Come on.

**Dask (he Poisons one of yours):** Poison's slow. I can wait.

**Dask (his first Ward springs):** Didn't see the trap? Nobody does.

**Dask (you have enough on the board to finish him):** Don't. Don't do that.

**Dask (if he wins):** _(He stares at the card in his hand.)_ Mam. Mam, it's —

**Dask (when you've beaten him):** No. _(He stares at the ring of light as it fades.)_ No, that was it. That was all of it.

**Dask:** My mam's note is with Ironvault. Eleven years. That card was the whole note.

_He unbuckles his deck box and sets it at your feet. The Shieldhound whines._

**Dask:** Caldus Morne wants that card. Do you understand? Everyone on this road's going to know your face.

_The cards fan out on screen: DASK'S DECK, thirty cards of hounds, traps and poison, yours by the Covenant._

**The player's choice:** Keep your hound. / Go home, Dask.

_(Keep your hound.) You take his Shieldhound's card out of the deck and hold it out._

**Dask (keep your hound):** _(He stares at it. He doesn't take it straight away.)_ ...Why?

_He takes it, and backs into the trees without another word._

**Dask (go home):** Home. _(It isn't a laugh.)_ Right.

_He goes into the trees._

**Bram:** _(To himself.)_ Morne.

_He keeps watch till dawn, and doesn't wake you for your turn._

**Caption:** Dawn

_White towers catch the first sun across the plains._

**Bram (at dawn):** Go through Dask's cards when we stop. Swap in what's nastier than what you've got. That's most of duelling.

**Bram:** There she is. Solhaven.

_He doesn't sound pleased._
