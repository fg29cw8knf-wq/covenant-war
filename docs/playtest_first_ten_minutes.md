# The first ten minutes: watching five new players

One page for Gary. The point is to find out where a new player gets stuck,
bored or confused before you spend more time on what comes after.

## Before you start

- Five people who have never seen the game. One at a time.
- The Mac or the iPhone, the newest build, sound on.
- Wipe the save first (or use a device the game has never run on), so they
  get the opening movie before the title, as a new player will.
- A phone timer, and this sheet, one copy per person.
- Say only this: "Play it however you like for ten minutes. I can't answer
  questions, but say out loud anything you're thinking." Then say nothing.
  If they ask for help, write an H and stay quiet. If they are truly stuck
  for a full minute, help, and write down exactly what you said.

## What to write down

Start the timer when they press New Game. Write the time at each line, and
a mark if one applies:

- **S** stuck for more than 10 seconds
- **H** asked for help
- **W** did the wrong thing first (say what)
- **+** leaned in, smiled, said something good

## Three questions afterwards

1. "What happened in the snow?" (Do they know they were Bram, and that the
   baby is the hero?)
2. "What's the game about?" (One sentence, their words.)
3. "What would you do next?" (Do they want to keep going?)

## What good looks like

- All five play the first card without help, within three minutes of New
  Game.
- Nobody is stuck for more than 30 seconds anywhere.
- At least four can say who Bram and the baby are.
- At least four want to keep playing.
