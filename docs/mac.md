# Playing the game on your Mac

## 1. Download

**https://github.com/fg29cw8knf-wq/covenant-war/releases/download/mac/CovenantWar-Mac.zip**

This link always gives the newest build: it's rebuilt by itself every time the
game's `main` branch changes (it takes a few minutes). The page
https://github.com/fg29cw8knf-wq/covenant-war/releases/tag/mac says when it was built.

## 2. Open it

Safari usually unzips it into **Downloads** as **The Covenant War**. If you see
`CovenantWar-Mac.zip` instead, double-click it. Then double-click **The Covenant War**.

## 3. The first time only

The app isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account yet, so macOS says it
can't check it and won't open it. To allow it:

1. Click **Done** (not Move to Bin).
2. Open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and scroll down to **Security**.
3. Next to "The Covenant War was blocked…", click **Open Anyway**, then enter your
   Mac password.
4. Double-click the game again and click **Open**.

After that it opens normally. Each new download asks once more.

The Mac app plays at full quality, with mouse, keyboard or a controller. Your
progress is saved on the Mac, separately from the iPhone. Since 23 October the
opening film plays at its full 4K through the Mac's own video player (about
420 MB of the download); click it for Skip, or press Esc.
