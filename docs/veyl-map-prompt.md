# The map of Veyl: the painting's prompt

For the last beat of Chapter 1, "Next time the game opens": dawn, the wagon
rolling out of Solhaven, Bram's "Pyreholt's nearest.", and "Choose a kingdom.
(Tap a banner on the map.)". Paint it whenever you like; the game puts the
names and the Champions' banners on top, so the painting has no words on it.

Where each place sits comes from the script where it says (marked **script**),
and is my suggestion where it doesn't (marked **proposed**). Change any of the
proposed ones before you paint and I'll move the labels to match.

- **The centre of Veyl** belongs to whoever holds the Throne (the world doc:
  "The centre of Veyl belongs to whoever holds the Throne"); the Convocation
  is held there. **script** for the centre; how it looks is **proposed**.
- **Solhaven**, the Sun Court, "the capital of the Age": just south of the
  centre. **proposed** (the script makes it the holder's city, not where).
- **Ashford** is west of Solhaven: the Prologue's caption is "The Thornwood
  road · east, towards Solhaven". A village in a valley, a river through it.
  **script**
- **The Thornwood** lies between Ashford and the plain; from its last ridge
  "the plain opens below, hazed... Solhaven's under it, two days on." **script**
- **The Shardlands** are uphill of Ashford ("the Sigilfall up in the
  Shardlands"; Ashford's stream runs cold from it; Bram walked down "the
  Shardlands road" to Ashford): a storm-scarred waste where the Sigils fall.
  **script** that it's above Ashford; north-west of it is **proposed**.
- **Pyreholt** is the nearest kingdom to Solhaven ("Pyreholt's nearest."), a
  forge-fortress in a dead volcano in black ash badlands, grain plains below
  its cliffs, and its steel goes "north and east" (to Hrimmark and
  Stormreach); its exiles take the "south road". South-west of Solhaven:
  **proposed**, to fit all of that.
- **Hrimmark** is north (Marda's son "went north"; "somewhere up north a gate
  comes down"): snow, an ice throne. **script**
- **Stormreach**, a sea power with ships: on the east coast. **proposed** (the
  steel goes east to it).
- **Glyphmere**, the scholars' kingdom, sits below snowy mountain passes (Bram:
  "A pass above Glyphmere, snow to the knees"): in the north-western
  mountains, on a high lake. **proposed**
- **Oriel's kingdom** (its name still to decide), the blindfolded priestess's:
  misty north-eastern highlands. **proposed**
- **Umbravel**, always the last kingdom the player reaches: the far
  south-east, as far from Ashford as Veyl goes. **proposed**
