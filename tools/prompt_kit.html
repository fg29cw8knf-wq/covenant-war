Card Prompt Kit

Every card in The Covenant War, round by round: what's already in the game, and a ready-to-paste prompt for each picture, model and piece of music still to make.

  1. Tap Copy and paste it into your tool. Where a prompt says to attach a finished painting, attach it, so the look matches.
  2. Save it at the size and file name shown on each prompt, and send it over. Any file name is fine: Claude renames it.
  3. Tap Mark sent to keep track. Your ticks stay in this browser; a job turns green once it's in the game.
Rites, Wards and Relics only need a card painting. Totems need a card painting, a battlefield cut-out and a 3D model. Summons need a card painting, a Summon cut-out and a 3D model, and the newest ones also a sky scene and their own music. Until a picture or model comes, the game shows a stand-in, so nothing breaks while you work through the list. The Chapter 1 cards are also in Solhaven: Your Step-by-Step.
