3D creature models live here as creatures/<card id>.glb and summons/<card id>.glb
(see the 3D Creature Models Plan doc). Until a model exists the game uses the
creature's cut-out picture.

Each model's colour atlas sits next to it as <card id>_atlas.png (2048x2048),
referenced from the GLB by file name rather than embedded, so Godot imports
the texture once with the settings in <card id>_atlas.png.import (lossy, with
mipmaps). To add or replace a model from a pack with an embedded 4K texture,
split it first:  python3 tools/split_glb.py <pack>/<id>.glb assets/models/creatures

Models face +Z with their feet at Y=0 and carry the clips idle, attack, hit,
defeat, spawn, run and victory. Material names drive the in-game shading:
surfaces called "fire", "mist" or "veil" are drawn additively as light.
